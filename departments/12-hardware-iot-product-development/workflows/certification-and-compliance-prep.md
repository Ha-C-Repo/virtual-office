# Workflow: Certification and Compliance Preparation (generic)

Applies before pilot and again before commercial launch.

1. Identify target markets and the regulatory regimes that apply (wireless emissions, product safety, battery, privacy, consumer protection). **Gate:** confirm with qualified professionals; this workflow does not decide applicability.
2. List required tests and documents; assign owners and timelines.
3. Freeze the hardware design revision used for testing (design lock).
4. Engage an accredited test lab through the principal.
5. Track results; failures return to engineering with a finding.
6. File certification records in the ops folder; do not state "certified" externally until documents exist.
7. Re-test when hardware or radio changes.
