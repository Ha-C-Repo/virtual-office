# Workflow: Investor Outreach

1. Define target investor list and stage fit. **Gate:** principal approves list.
2. Pre-disclosure check with department 11.
3. Draft one-pager; later the deck and follow-ups (`../skills/investor-communications/SKILL.md`).
4. **Gate (sign-off):** principal approves every send; counsel reviews if it resembles an offering document.
5. NDA before technical detail; log disclosures.
6. Track meetings and follow-ups in a simple log.
7. Debrief after each meeting: questions asked, answers needed, risk to claims made.
