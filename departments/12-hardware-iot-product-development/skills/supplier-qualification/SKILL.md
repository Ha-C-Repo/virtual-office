# Supplier Qualification

## When to use
Selecting a component supplier, contract manufacturer, test lab or logistics provider, and on annual review.

## Inputs
`../../templates/supplier-scorecard.md`, quotes, sample results, references the principal provides, supplier documents (quality certificates, insurance, NDA).

## Steps
1. Define needs: part or service, volume, quality level, lead time, location constraints.
2. Request the same information from each candidate; record what was and was not provided.
3. Check an NDA and IP terms exist before sharing designs (`11-ip-patent-support`).
4. Score each criterion on the scorecard; record evidence beside each score.
5. Identify single-source risks and a backup option for each critical part.
6. Order samples through the principal; record sample test results.

## Outputs
Completed scorecard, risk list, recommendation to the principal.

## Gates
Principal approves any supplier before an order. Counsel reviews supply and manufacturing agreements.
