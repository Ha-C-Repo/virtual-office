# Product Area Handoff

## Steps
1. Each area owner updates `../../templates/product-area-status.md` weekly.
2. List interface changes (API, message formats, pin or connector changes, app permissions) and the areas they affect.
3. Any interface change that breaks another area needs a design lock first.
4. Product lead reviews, resolves conflicts, updates the roadmap.
5. Open decisions go to the principal with a recommendation.
