# Connected Product Risk Review

This is a generic checklist, not engineering or legal sign-off.

## Security
Device identity and secure provisioning; signed firmware and over-the-air updates with rollback; key storage; encrypted transport; authentication between device, cloud and mobile; least privilege; logging; vulnerability disclosure and patch process; end-of-life plan.

## Privacy
Data minimization; location and usage data consent; retention schedule; user access and deletion; privacy notices; applicable privacy laws.

## Safety
Failure modes and fail-safe behavior, especially if the device interacts with vehicles, locks, power or people; test evidence; incident process.

## Regulatory (identify applicable regimes with professionals)
Radio emissions and wireless certification (for example FCC rules in the US, CE marking in Europe), product safety standards, battery transport rules, consumer protection, automotive-related requirements where relevant.

## Output
Risk table: risk, area, likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner, status. Items needing external professionals are flagged for the principal.
