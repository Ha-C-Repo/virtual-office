# Investor Communications

## Formats
- **One-pager:** problem, solution, why now, market, traction (only verified), team roles, ask. Template: `../../templates/investor-one-pager.md`.
- **Deck, 8-10 slides:** `../../templates/investor-deck-outline.md`.
- **Follow-up email:** `../../templates/investor-followup-email.md`.

## Steps
1. Confirm the audience and stage.
2. Run the pre-disclosure check (department 11): what technical detail is safe at this stage; NDA status.
3. Draft with placeholders for every metric; fill only verified figures.
4. Mark confidentiality legends.
5. Principal approves; counsel reviews anything that looks like a securities offering statement.
6. Log what was sent and to whom.
