# Market Strategy Brief

## Steps
1. Who has the problem? Segment and describe the buyer and the user (they may differ).
2. Value proposition in the customer's terms.
3. Channel options: direct, distributors, integrators, enterprise or fleet sales; partnership types.
4. Business model: hardware sale, subscription, service fees, mix; model assumptions labeled.
5. Competition from public information only, dated.
6. Brand: name direction (send names to department 11 before use), tone, proof points that are verified.
7. Risks and what to test first in the pilot.

Output: a two-page brief for the principal. Business model numbers are placeholders until validated ({{PRICE}}, {{UNIT_COST}}).
