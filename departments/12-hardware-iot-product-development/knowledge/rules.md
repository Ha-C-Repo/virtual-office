# Rules: Hardware and IoT Product Development

1. Decisions that cross product areas are recorded as design locks; superseded, never edited.
2. Verify, do not generate: no unverified specifications, certifications, partners, customers, traction or forecasts. Targets are labeled targets.
3. IP first: pre-disclosure check and NDA before any technical detail leaves the company.
4. Launch phase transitions need evidence and principal approval.
5. Security: signed firmware, secure update path with rollback, unique device identity, no secrets in code or records.
6. Privacy: consent for location and usage data, minimal retention, access and deletion paths.
7. Safety and regulatory applicability are confirmed by qualified professionals; this department only organizes the work.
8. Investor communications: drafted here, approved by the principal, reviewed by counsel where they may be offering statements.
9. Confidentiality folder and domain operations records are restricted; credentials are never recorded.
10. Pilot agreements and customer terms are reviewed by counsel.
