# Regulatory Map for Connected Products (generic orientation)

Not legal or engineering advice. Applicability depends on product, radios, battery, market and use. Qualified professionals and an accredited lab decide (VERIFY). Use this table to ask better questions.

| Topic | Typical question to ask | Examples of regimes to ask about |
|---|---|---|
| Radio emissions and wireless | Does the product transmit (cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, other)? | FCC rules (US), CE/RED (EU), national equivalents |
| Electromagnetic compatibility and safety | Is it mains or battery powered? | Product safety standards, EMC standards |
| Battery | Lithium cells? Shipping? | Battery transport and safety rules |
| Privacy | Does it collect location, usage or personal data? | Privacy and consumer-protection laws where users live |
| Children | Could children use it? | Children's privacy laws |
| Vehicle-adjacent | Does it connect to a vehicle bus or control anything? | Automotive-related requirements, product liability review |
| Environmental | Hazardous substances, recycling? | RoHS, WEEE style rules |
| Labeling and marking | What marks and statements must the product carry? | Per regime above |
| Cybersecurity | Is a baseline or label required in target markets? | National and regional device security rules |

Output: a list of questions for the principal and the lab, with a column for who answered and the date.
