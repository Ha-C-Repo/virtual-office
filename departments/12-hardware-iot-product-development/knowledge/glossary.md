# Glossary: Hardware and IoT Product Development

- **Design lock / ADR:** numbered record of an architecture decision, frozen until superseded.
- **Product areas:** cloud, firmware, hardware, mobile, website.
- **Launch phases:** pre-launch, pilot, commercial, scale.
- **OTA update:** over-the-air firmware update.
- **Provisioning:** giving each device its identity and credentials securely.
- **Device identity:** unique, verifiable identifier and keys for a device.
- **Fleet:** the deployed population of devices.
- **Telematics:** remote collection of vehicle or equipment data.
- **BOM:** bill of materials.
- **EVT / DVT / PVT:** engineering, design and production validation test builds.
- **Certification:** formal regulatory or standards approval from accredited testing.
- **One-pager:** single-page investor summary.
- **NDA marking:** confidentiality legend on shared material.
- **Pilot agreement:** written terms for limited-scale trial use.
- **End of life:** retirement plan for devices and data.
