## Steps
1. Log the review (`../../templates/reputation-review-queue.md`).
2. Classify: class 1 allegation (escalate, no draft), class 2 complaint, class 3 positive naming a staff action, class 4 generic.
3. Class 1: write the escalation note (`../../templates/reputation-escalation-note.md`) and stop.
4. Others: draft from `../../templates/reputation-response-by-class.md`.
5. Compliance check if any vehicle fact or financing claim appears.
6. Owner voice check. Owner approves and posts.
7. Update the queue. A complaint closes only on a documented resolution.

## Rules
No defensiveness, no re-litigating, no admission of a legal claim, no unauthorized promise. Do not claim a review was removed. Do not reuse praise as an ad testimonial without permission.
