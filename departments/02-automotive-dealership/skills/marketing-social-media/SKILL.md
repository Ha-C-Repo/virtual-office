## Steps
1. Platform profile check per account: license displayed, legal name on account, commerce policy recorded. Never checked is not compliant.
2. Draft (`../../templates/marketing-social-caption.md`) and tag six dimensions at draft time: format, hook, topic, media, length, cta. Topics: specific unit, financing and approval, service and recon, staff and lot, customer delivery, community.
3. Screen through `../marketing-vehicle-ad-compliance/SKILL.md` and `../marketing-owner-voice-check/SKILL.md`.
4. Queue for owner approval. Lifecycle: drafted, screened, queued, approved, scheduled, published, measured.
5. Publish by driving the platform's own native scheduler in the owner's logged-in browser at human pace, one task at a time. Halt on captcha, checkpoint or rate warning until a named human clears it. Never solve captchas or circumvent limits.
6. Ingest first-party analytics exports (not screen scraping); keep originals; mark series breaks.
7. Learning: fast lane (weekly, large effects, qualitative reading); slow lane (cumulative Beta posteriors per dimension and level, Thompson sampling for next week's slots). Report "not separated" when evidence is insufficient. About 40 posts per arm are needed to detect roughly an 18 percent lift.

## Red flags
No auto-reply to comments or DMs. No third-party scheduler (short analytics retention). Paid promotion is out of scope.
