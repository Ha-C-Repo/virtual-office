## Order of preference
1. Slow pan-and-zoom over real lot photos with local assembly. Nothing generated, nothing to disclose.
2. Image-to-video from the dealership's own photos.
3. Generation only when stills cannot carry the message; log it in the AI disclosure register.

## Rules
- Brief first (`../../templates/marketing-video-brief.md`); never write prompts before a shot list.
- Script timing: 15 s voiceover 32 to 37 words; 30 s 65 to 75; 60 s 110 to 130. Hook in 3 seconds; call to action in the final 5.
- Composite all stats, lower-thirds, wordmarks, URLs and phone numbers in post. No readable text generated in frame.
- One idea per clip. Max 6 seconds per generated clip.
- Cost gate: quote credits and wait for an explicit go before any paid generation.
- Presenter and voice cloning are HELD pending recorded likeness consent. Cloned voice is media, never telephony.
- Owner approves before anything is public.
