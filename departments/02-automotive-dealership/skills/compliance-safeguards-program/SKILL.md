## Program elements
- Written information-security program with a named qualified individual.
- Multi-factor authentication; encryption at rest and in transit.
- Vendor oversight, including confirmation that sub-processors do not train on client data.
- Incident response plan.
- Destruction via crypto-erase on solid-state storage with certificate and hash.

## Rules
- Credit applications, licenses, pay stubs, SSNs and bank details never go through unpaid AI APIs or consumer chat. Lot photos are fine on any tier.
- Re-verify commercial-use terms of any AI tier before using it.
- Record each document in the data processing register.
- Keys and credentials are never in output.

## Steps
Review annually and after any incident; track gaps as compliance scorecard items.
