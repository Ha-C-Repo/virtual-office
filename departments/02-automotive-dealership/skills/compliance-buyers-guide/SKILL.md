## Rules
- A buyer's guide is on every used vehicle, displayed on the vehicle, matching the sales contract.
- Bilingual version where the sale is negotiated in another language.
- The warranty and as-is boxes are never both checked.
- Ad warranty language matches the guide word for word. Warranty terms {{WARRANTY_TERMS}} must reconcile.
- "As is" does not cure an affirmative misrepresentation under the state deceptive-trade-practices act.

## Steps
1. Generate the guide from the unit record and warranty terms.
2. Compare listing, contract and guide.
3. Record in the deal jacket and disclosure register.
4. Flag mismatches as gate failures.
