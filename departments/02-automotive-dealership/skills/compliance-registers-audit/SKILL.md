## Registers
1. Ad substantiation: no row, no claim; a row does not clear a superlative; 90-day re-verify.
2. AI disclosure: conversational surfaces and AI-involved assets.
3. Data processing: where sensitive documents live, retention expiry, destruction certificate.
4. Disclosure (vehicle condition): defect missing from register, guide or listing is a gate failure; acknowledgment before DELIVERED.
5. Platform policy: quarterly re-check; UNVERIFIED rows cannot support a claim.

## Steps
1. Open each register template (`../../templates/compliance-*.md`).
2. Flag empty required fields, expired verification dates, rows without evidence.
3. Cross-check live ads and posts against the substantiation register.
4. Update the scorecard (`../../templates/compliance-scorecard.md`).
5. List actions with owner and date.
