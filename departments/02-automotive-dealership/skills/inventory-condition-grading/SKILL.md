---
name: inventory-condition-grading
description: Assigns grade A, B or C from evidence on file and re-runs when new evidence arrives. The grade gates which adjectives copy may use.
---
## Grades
- **A, Inspected:** full inspection sheet, service records, clean title history, all recon closed. Specific claims tied to line items; warranty stated in full.
- **B, Declared:** VIN decode, auction condition report and full photo set. Factual description, defects named, no adjectives beyond the report's.
- **C, As-Is Unverified:** photos and decode only. Nouns and numbers only. As-is box checked and the ad says so.
- Floor is C. No photo and no decode means not gradeable.

## Steps
1. List evidence on file with confidence.
2. Apply the criteria top-down.
3. An open recon item downgrades an otherwise-A unit.
4. Record the grade, evidence list and date.
5. Re-run after recon closes or new documents arrive.

## Outputs
Grade, reasons, allowed-adjective list for Marketing.

## Gates
Grade is recorded but never moves price arithmetic. Conflicting evidence is resolved, not averaged.
