## Rules
- Run the covered-borrower check on every deal regardless of buyer self-report. Indeterminate is treated as covered.
- The annualized rate cap applies to covered borrowers. The purchase-money exclusion is lost if cash is advanced beyond the price or certain add-ons are financed.
- Mandatory arbitration ban applies.
- Servicemember relief act: pre-service rate cap and court-order requirement for repossession (immediate with in-house paper).
- A base off-limits designation by a disciplinary control board is a business-ending risk. Predicate is a pattern: unmanaged concealment-flavored reviews plus universal-approval ads. Parameter: {{NEARBY_INSTALLATION}}; the rule is unconditional.

## Steps
1. Record the check result and date on the deal and in the jacket.
2. If covered: re-structure within the cap or confirm the exclusion applies.
3. Flag the structure to the owner before submission.
4. Feed reputation escalations that touch this exposure to Compliance.

## Gates
Check recorded on 100 percent of funded deals. Escalation stops the deal.
