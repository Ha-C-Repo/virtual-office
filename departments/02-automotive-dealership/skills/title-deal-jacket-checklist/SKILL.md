## Required sets
- **All deals:** buyer's guide matching the contract, executed odometer statement, assigned title with transfer application, covered-borrower check record.
- **Outside finance or in-house paper add:** credit application and approval, retail installment contract, stipulations satisfied, adverse-action notice if any, lien perfected.
- **In-house adds:** servicing terms.

## Steps
1. Select the checklist by deal type (`../../templates/title-deal-jacket-checklist.md`).
2. Mark each document PRESENT, MISSING or NOT_APPLICABLE with reason.
3. Daily sweep: list deals with MISSING items and age.
4. At funding, block if any required item is MISSING.

## Outputs
Jacket status per deal, sweep list.

## Gates
Missing document at funding escalates to the owner.
