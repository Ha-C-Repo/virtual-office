# 07 Reputation: Digital Footprint Runbook

Reusable method for a new or re-baselined dealership. Each step is phrased as the question a new customer would ask. Order matters. Do not skip ahead.

## Steps
1. **Can they find the real business?** Claim and verify the business profile on the search engine (postcard or other verification). Record who holds the role. Do not reply to existing reviews until claimed.
2. **Are the facts right?** Record a baseline: rating, count, unanswered reviews, hours, phone, address, website. Treat older findings as historical and re-baseline on first live access.
3. **Is there one front door?** Choose one authoritative domain ({{WEBSITE}}). Redirect or retire others. Update every listing link.
4. **Is the inventory real and current?** Get inventory live and syndicated, reconcile live vs ground (`01-inventory-vehicle-record-to-listing.md`).
5. **Is the brand on a business Page?** Move marketing from any personal profile to the business Page. Do not delete old content. Confirm license displayed and legal name on the account (`../templates/compliance-platform-policy-register.md`).
6. **Do they answer reviews?** Start reputation response (`07-reputation-review-response.md`). Log each review: date, source, issue category, proposed owner, next action, status.
7. **Do they ask for reviews fairly?** Invite honest reviews consistently, no cherry-picking.

## Rules
- Zero added spend for the first plan.
- Never state that reviews were removed.
- Never promise a rating increase.
- Track new reviews, time to triage, human-posted replies, open complaints and documented resolutions.
