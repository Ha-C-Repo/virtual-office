# 04 Title and Admin: Deal Jacket and Deadlines

Owner: Title and Admin agent (`../agents/04-title-admin-agent.md`). Human owner: {{TITLE_CLERK}}. Skills: `../skills/title-title-and-tag/SKILL.md`, `title-deal-jacket-checklist`, `title-deadline-risk-detector`.

## At sale (per deal)
1. Open the deadline timeline (`../templates/title-deadline-timeline.md`). Fill anchor dates. Unknown day counts are NEEDS_INPUT.
2. Execute and file the odometer statement.
3. Issue a temp tag if needed and log it (`../templates/title-temp-tag-log.md`).
4. Submit the title application. Record the filing date. For financed deals, open a row in `../templates/title-lien-filing-tracker.md`.
5. Build the jacket against `../templates/title-deal-jacket-checklist.md` for the deal type.

## Daily sweep (deal jacket compliance sweep)
1. For every open deal: missing documents, title clock, temp tag expiry, stipulations, covered-borrower check recorded, buyer's guide on file and matching the contract.
2. Warning levels: URGENT at 3 days, THIS WEEK at 7. "Filed" is not "recorded".
3. Check anchor dates; missing anchor dates raise dependency risk earlier.
4. Report to the morning briefing (`00-morning-briefing.md`).

## Office-level (monthly review)
Dealer license renewal ({{LICENSE_EXPIRY}}), continuing education per licensed person, bond and insurance certificate dates. Alerts at 30, 15 and 3 days (`../templates/compliance-scorecard.md`).

## Gates and escalation
- No deal is reported funded with a jacket missing a required document.
- A deadline or tag inside its warning window with no filed action: escalate to {{OWNER}}.
- Zero transfers past deadline. License renewal filed before expiry.

## Human sign-off
{{TITLE_CLERK}} or {{OWNER}} files with the state and signs. The office prepares and tracks.
