# 06 Marketing: Ad and Social Publishing

Owner: Marketing agent (`../agents/06-marketing-agent.md`). Skills: `../skills/marketing-vehicle-ad-compliance/SKILL.md`, `marketing-social-media`, `marketing-owner-voice-check`, `marketing-video-walkaround`. Organic only. Business Page only, never a personal profile. Nothing auto-posts, auto-replies or auto-dials.

## Steps
1. **Plan.** Weekly content mix across topic levels: specific unit, financing and approval, service and recon, staff and lot, customer delivery, community. Use only verified, available inventory.
2. **Draft** with dimension tags set at draft time (`../templates/marketing-social-caption.md`): format, hook, topic, media, length, cta.
3. **Media.** Real photos or video of the real unit. Video follows `../templates/marketing-video-brief.md`; walkaround is pan and zoom over real photos. Never a generated vehicle, building or person.
4. **Substantiate.** Every claim needs a row in `../templates/compliance-ad-substantiation-register.md`.
5. **Screen.** Vehicle-ad compliance (block = law, warn = copy quality), specificity quota (year, mileage, price or stock number), grade-adjective gate, spoken-script gate for video. Non-English copy is refused until a named bilingual reviewer is recorded.
6. **Voice check.**
7. **Approval queue.** {{OWNER}} approves.
8. **Publish.** A human, or the platform's own scheduler driven at human pace in the owner's logged-in browser. Halt on any captcha, checkpoint or rate warning until a named human clears it. Never solve a captcha. No third-party scheduler.
9. **Measure.** Import first-party platform exports; keep originals. Report honestly: if the audience is too small to separate signal, say "not separated".
10. **Platform policy re-check** quarterly (`../templates/compliance-platform-policy-register.md`).

## Rules
- Any payment or rate in a caption drags the full disclosure block; usually leave it out.
- No auto-replies to comments or messages. Financing questions go to {{FI_MANAGER}}.
- Unit labels used internally are never public. Never backdate.

## Escalation
A claim with no substantiation row; any generated vehicle, person or building (hard block to {{OWNER}} and Compliance).
