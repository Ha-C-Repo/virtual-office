# Review Response by Class

Every public reply passes the owner voice check. A reply that touches a vehicle fact or financing claim also passes the compliance check in marketing context (strictest). A human approves and posts. Reply only after the business profile is claimed.

Voice: short sentences, plain verbs, no em-dashes, no emojis, no filler openers, no hedging, no three-adjective lists. Sign with role only: {{OWNER_ROLE}}, {{DEALERSHIP}}.

## Class 1. Concealment, safety or title allegation
Do not draft a public reply. Use `reputation-escalation-note.md`. Owner decides.

## Class 2. Service or follow-up complaint, no concealment claim
Draft:
> {{REVIEWER_FIRST_NAME}}, thank you for telling us. {{SPECIFIC_ISSUE_RESTATED_IN_ONE_SENTENCE}}. I want to make this right. Please call {{PHONE}} and ask for {{OWNER_ROLE}}, or send a message to {{CONTACT_CHANNEL}}. {{OWNER_ROLE}}, {{DEALERSHIP}}

Rules: acknowledge the specific issue, invite direct contact, no defensiveness, no re-litigating, no admission of a legal claim, no promise the owner has not approved. Do not count the complaint resolved because a reply was posted. Log the resolution in the queue.

## Class 3. Positive review naming a specific staff action
Draft:
> {{REVIEWER_FIRST_NAME}}, thank you. {{SPECIFIC_ACTION_PRAISED}} is what we aim for on every sale. We are glad {{VEHICLE_OR_SERVICE_REFERENCE}} works for you. {{OWNER_ROLE}}, {{DEALERSHIP}}

Do not reuse the review as an ad testimonial without written permission.

## Class 4. Generic positive or negative
Draft (positive):
> Thank you, {{REVIEWER_FIRST_NAME}}. We appreciate it. {{OWNER_ROLE}}, {{DEALERSHIP}}

Draft (generic negative, no detail given):
> {{REVIEWER_FIRST_NAME}}, we are sorry we fell short. Please call {{PHONE}} so we can learn what happened. {{OWNER_ROLE}}, {{DEALERSHIP}}

Low priority. Still human-reviewed.

## Never
Quote a price, rate or payment. Name another customer. Mention a deal detail in public. Say a review was removed. Promise a rating.
