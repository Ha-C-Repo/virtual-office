# Investor Reporting

Agent: investor relations writer. Skills: `../skills/investor-reporting/SKILL.md`, `../skills/investor-package/SKILL.md`, `../skills/grant-application/SKILL.md`.

## Steps
1. Period close: audits, cost register, reserve log and counts complete.
2. Finance controller confirms figures and count status.
3. Draft the update (`../templates/investor-update.md`) or package (`../templates/investor-package-outline.md`).
4. Output scrubber and voice check.
5. GATE: {{OWNER_ROLE}} approves.
6. Counsel reviews offer terms or grant certifications when present.
7. {{OWNER_ROLE}} sends. Log in the changelog.
