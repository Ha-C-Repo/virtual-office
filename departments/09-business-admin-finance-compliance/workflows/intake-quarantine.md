# Intake and Quarantine

Agent: compliance officer. Related: `../skills/trade-documentation-log/SKILL.md`, `../skills/permit-tracker/SKILL.md`.

## Steps
1. Before arrival: confirm the trade documentation row is complete and the permit is in force.
2. On arrival: staged ingress treatment per the SOP. Record each treatment step with staff ID.
3. Move to the quarantine system, isolated from production. Quarantine minimum days depend on tier.
4. Inspection record: intake date, asset ID, type, source reference, trade document reference, tier, treatments, quarantine system, days required, release date, inspector.
5. Release gate: only on or after the release date, with inspector sign-off.
6. Pre-integration treatment before moving into production.
7. Log everything to the intake register.

## Gates and sign-off
No release before the clock. Inspector signs. {{OWNER_ROLE}} approves exceptions in writing. Incidents go to the incident log.
