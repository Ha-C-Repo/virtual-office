# Friday Reserve Sweep

Skill: `../skills/reserve-sweep/SKILL.md`. Log: `../templates/reserve-sweep-log.md`.

## Steps
1. Confirm the Monday audit for the week is complete and sales data is fresh.
2. Read gross revenue from the sales register.
3. Apply {{SWEEP_PERCENT}}; check the balance against {{RESERVE_CAP}}.
4. Write the instruction and the log row.
5. GATE: {{OWNER_ROLE}} approves and executes the transfer. The AI never moves money.
6. After execution, the records keeper updates the log status.
