# Voice check

## When to use
Every draft. Silent unless there are violations.

## Registers
Internal protocol (terse, imperative), buyer (warm, specific, no hype), investor (plain, numeric, sourced), owner (direct). See `../../templates/brand-voice-guide.md`.

## Block list
Dashes used as punctuation; "not just X, it is Y"; three-adjective stacks; filler openers; hedging; unresolved placeholders; invented claims.

## Warn list
Long sentences; jargon in buyer text; adjective-heavy phrasing.

## Email conventions
Subject states the point; salutation by first name placeholder; body in short paragraphs; one ask; consistent signature block.

## Outputs
Block with lines, warn list, or silent pass.
