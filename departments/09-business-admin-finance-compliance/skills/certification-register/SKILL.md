# Certification register

## When to use
Onboarding, quarterly review, and before assigning a task that requires a certification level.

## Steps
1. Per staff role record: level, role, scope of tasks permitted, pay tier, SOP sign-offs, PPE issued.
2. Before assigning a task, check that the staff member's level covers it. If not, flag.
3. Track the onboarding boot camp checklist and dates.
4. Pay tier changes require {{OWNER_ROLE}} approval and are logged.

## Outputs
Register rows (`../../templates/certification-register.md`).

## Gates
Pay data is internal only and never appears in buyer or investor documents.
