# Investor reporting

## When to use
At each reporting period (monthly or quarterly).

## Steps
1. Close the period: confirm the yield audits, cost register, reserve log and counts for the period.
2. Compare to prior period and to plan; explain variances in plain words.
3. Draft the update with `../../templates/investor-update.md`.
4. Disclose problems at the same weight as wins.
5. Scrub and tag every figure source.

## Outputs
Update draft and an internal reconciliation note.

## Gates
{{OWNER_ROLE}} approves. Figures that changed after a previous report are flagged as restated.
