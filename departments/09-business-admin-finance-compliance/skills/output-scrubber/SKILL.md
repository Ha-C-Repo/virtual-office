# Output scrubber

## When to use
Before any buyer-facing or investor-facing document leaves the office.

## Checklist
1. Supplier and vendor names removed.
2. Internal costs, margins and staff pay removed.
3. Facility layout, addresses of production areas and security details removed.
4. Health or survival language only from the approved buyer guarantee policy.
5. Audience correct: internal, buyer or investor (`../../knowledge/rules.md`).
6. Voice check passed (`../voice-check/SKILL.md`).
7. Placeholders resolved.

## Outputs
PASS, AUTO-FIX (list changes) or BLOCK.

## Gates
Anything blocked returns to the author. {{OWNER_ROLE}} approves everything external.
