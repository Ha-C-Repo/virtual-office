# Brand Voice Guide: {{COMPANY}}

## Registers
- Internal protocol: terse, imperative, numbered steps, exact values.
- Buyer: warm, specific, honest about what the asset is, no hype.
- Investor: plain, numeric, sourced, risks stated.
- Owner: direct, short, decision first.

## Always
Lead with the point. Use specific numbers with source. Name the next step. One ask per message.

## Never
Dashes as punctuation; "not just X, it is Y"; three-adjective stacks; filler openers; unsourced superlatives; health or survival claims outside the approved policy.

## Listing structure (buyer)
Title; identification; size or stage; price; hold or availability status; shipping and policy link; photo requirement.

## Email
Subject: states the point. Salutation: {{FIRST_NAME}}. Body: short paragraphs. Signature: {{SIGNATURE_BLOCK}}.

## Text messages
Under 300 characters, one ask, include opt-out where required.
