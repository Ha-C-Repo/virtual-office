# 03 Tour Build

Owner: tour-producer | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Listing intake complete (`02-listing-intake.md`).
2. Asset harvest: every asset tagged with its source.
3. Floor plan read into a room graph (`../skills/floorplan-reading/SKILL.md`).
4. Layout inference (grade B only): all dimensions LOW confidence.
5. Model build: room graph, coordinate model, 3D mesh. GATE: computed area must reconcile to stated GLA within {{AREA_TOLERANCE_PCT}} percent.
6. Staging: image generation conditioned on real photos, labeled "AI-generated, illustrative, not a survey or measurement."
7. Tour composer: walkthrough page, waypoints, hotspots, mini-map, fly-through video.
8. Completeness check (`../skills/tour-build-and-check/SKILL.md`).
9. Publish gate: broker identification present on ads, absent in the MLS tour field; owner sign-off.
10. Move from working to final folder.

## Outputs
Graded tour package, completeness report, publish record.
