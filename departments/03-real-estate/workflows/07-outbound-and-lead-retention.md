# 07 Outbound and Lead Retention

Owner: lead-retention-manager | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Every outbound message enters one door. No side channels.
2. Check recorded owner decisions for the contact.
3. Check opt-outs and the internal do-not-call list. An opt-out on any channel applies to all channels, permanently.
4. Check the consent ledger for the channel: basis, date, scope.
5. Channel content gate: email meets CAN-SPAM; SMS and similar meet TCPA consent or established business relationship; direct mail is open.
6. Retention calendar touches: verify the verified-on stamp and the year. GATE: stale data is refused.
7. Annual equity letter: `../templates/annual-equity-letter.md`; no net-to-seller math.
8. Queue with the gate report. SIGN-OFF: owner approves each item.
9. After send: record outbound events and outcomes (replies, appointments, listings).
10. Cold outbound (default off): requires the evidence pack (registry downloads, scrub freshness, training records, violation log). GATE: incomplete evidence means no list is written.

## Outputs
Gate reports, queue entries, ledger updates, monthly retention summary.
