# 08 Video Production

Owner: marketing-coordinator | Production: `../../07-ai-video-creative-production/` | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Confirm brand and formats: preroll, commercial, brand film, explainer, product demo, vertical reel.
2. Creative brief (`../templates/video-script-brief.md`).
3. Script.
4. Script screen: fair housing, disclosure, sourced facts, AI labeling. GATE: runs between SCRIPT and PROMPTS.
5. Prompts, then generation in the production department.
6. Assembly in the working folder. Final folder is written only after sign-off.
7. Check against the anti-"AI look" list: no unnatural faces or hands, no fake signage, no invented architecture presented as real.
8. SIGN-OFF: owner approves before public release.
9. Avatar presenters and cloned voices are for media only, never telephony, and require written consent of the person represented.

## Outputs
Approved final video, script and screen report.
