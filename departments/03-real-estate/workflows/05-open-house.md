# 05 Open House

Owner: marketing-coordinator | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Confirm listing, date, time, host, and seller permission.
2. Build the kit (`../skills/open-house-kit/SKILL.md`): sign-in sheet with consent line, flyer, feature sheet, tour QR or link, follow-up sequence, internal notes.
3. GATE: `../skills/listing-compliance/SKILL.md` on every public piece (fair housing, broker identification, sourced facts).
4. SIGN-OFF: owner approves print and posting.
5. Event day: host uses the sign-in sheet and the traffic log.
6. Within one day: enter visitors into lead files, record consent per channel.
7. Same evening and day 2: queue the follow-up messages through the outbound gate. Owner approves.
8. Debrief: feedback themes to the seller via the principal. Update `../templates/handoff-note.md`.
