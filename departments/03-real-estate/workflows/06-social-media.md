# 06 Social Media

Owner: marketing-coordinator | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Draft: caption, first comment, hashtags, media, dimension tags.
2. Script screen (`../skills/listing-compliance/SKILL.md`). GATE: blocked captions are logged and never queued.
3. Approval queue. SIGN-OFF: owner approves each post.
4. Publish one post at a time through the platform's native scheduler, at human pace. Stop and hand to a human at login, MFA or CAPTCHA.
5. Record the permalink and publish time.
6. Import platform analytics exports (CSV with a banner line before columns). Reconcile to posts. Flag missing fields and series breaks.
7. Weekly learning loop: cumulative results by content dimension; propose next week's mix. The loop proposes only; it never publishes.
8. Monthly profile check: broker name present and prominent, disclosure links in place.

## Outputs
Posted content log, metrics table, weekly mix proposal.
