# Social Media Loop

## When to use
Planning, drafting, publishing or reviewing social posts for the brokerage or a listing.

## Inputs
Content idea or listing, brand voice guide, account list, platform analytics exports (CSV, with a banner line before the metric columns).

## Steps
1. Draft caption, first comment, hashtags, media list. Tag content dimensions (format, topic, length, call to action).
2. Screen with `../listing-compliance/SKILL.md`. Blocked captions are logged and never queued.
3. Queue for approval. The owner approves each post.
4. Publish one post at a time through the native scheduler at human pace. Record the permalink. Stop and hand to a human at any login, MFA or CAPTCHA.
5. Import platform exports; reconcile metrics to posts; flag missing fields and series breaks.
6. Weekly: summarize results by content dimension and propose next week's mix. Proposals are advisory; the loop never publishes by itself.
7. Verify profile checks: broker name present and prominent, disclosure links present.

## Outputs
Approved posts, permalink log, weekly learning note.

## Gates
Human-paced only; no bulk posting; no fabricated testimonials or metrics. Metrics shown must come from imported exports.
