# Buyer Consult

## When to use
A new buyer client, or an existing buyer moving to showings or an offer.

## Inputs
Buyer needs (budget range, timeline, must-haves, areas), pre-approval letter status, candidate listings.

## Steps
1. Intake: ask one question at a time; record needs as the buyer states them.
2. Confirm pre-approval is current before showings; record the expiry date as data.
3. Build a tour itinerary by listing and route using sourced facts only.
4. Offer support: pull comps via `../cma-pricing/SKILL.md`; show the corridor and a range; the buyer and principal decide the number.
5. Inspection triage: sort findings into safety, major systems, cosmetic; draft the repair request via `../amendment-generator/SKILL.md`.
6. Never describe neighborhoods by who lives there. Use facts: schools data with source, distances, amenities.

## Outputs
Buyer profile, itinerary, offer comp summary, triage list.

## Gates
Fair housing applies to buyers: no steering. The principal reviews every offer number. Communications to the buyer go through the outbound gate.
