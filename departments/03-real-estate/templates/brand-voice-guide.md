# Brand Voice Guide: {{COMPANY}}

Tagline: {{TAGLINE}}

## Voice
Warm, direct, plain. Short sentences. Concrete facts. Write like a trusted local professional talking to one person.

## Banned patterns
- Em-dashes (use a period or comma)
- Three-adjective lists
- "Not just X, it's Y" constructions
- Vague intensifiers (very, truly, incredibly, stunning, must-see)
- Cliches ("won't last long", "dream home", "turn-key" without specifics)
- Emojis in client documents
- Unfilled tokens such as `{{...}}`
- Stock openers and closers ("I hope this finds you well", "Great question!")

## Formatting rules
- Lead with the point.
- Numbers as digits; units stated; dates in {{DATE_FORMAT}}.
- One call to action per message.
- Listing copy: facts first (beds, baths, GLA with source, lot, year, upgrades), then one sentence on the setting.

## Fair housing reminder
Describe the property, never the people. See `fair-housing-rewrite-table.md`.

## Examples
| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Stunning, spacious, sun-drenched home | 4 bedrooms, 2,100 sq ft per county record, south-facing living room |
| Perfect for a growing family | Four bedrooms and a fenced yard |
