# 13 Storm Surge Response

Owner: roofing-operations-director | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Event detected. Open the event log (`../templates/storm-response-lead-log.md`) and read `../skills/storm-surge-playbook/SKILL.md`.
2. Set capacity: inspections and tarps per day; extra phone coverage; sub support only if vetted.
3. GATE (human): owner approves the status message and any pricing policy. Normal price book applies unless the owner decides otherwise with legal limits checked (VERIFY).
4. Triage leads: open roof or active leak first, then inspections, then information (`../skills/lead-intake-triage/SKILL.md`).
5. Tarp jobs with written authorization and photos (`10-warranty-and-service-call.md` steps 7 and 8).
6. Inspections and claims support (`02-inspection-to-estimate.md`, `04-insurance-claim-support.md`).
7. Marketing and canvassing only after the compliance gate (`../skills/review-and-referral-compliance/SKILL.md`, `../templates/canvass-script.md`). No deductible mentions.
8. Daily review: backlog days, response time, crew load. Pause new slots above {{MAX_BACKLOG_DAYS}} days and tell leads honestly.
9. After the surge: debrief, lessons, update this playbook, KPI snapshot.

## Outputs
Event log, daily capacity plan, tarp record, debrief.
