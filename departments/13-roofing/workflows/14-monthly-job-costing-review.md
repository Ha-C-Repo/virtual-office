# 14 Monthly Job-Costing Review

Owner: finance-controller | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Confirm every cost is tagged to a project each week; list untagged items.
2. Roll up completed and active jobs (`../skills/job-costing-and-margin-review/SKILL.md`, `../templates/job-cost-report.md`) with `../harnesses/job_cost.py`.
3. Compute overhead per billable hour and margin versus markup with the harness.
4. Compare actual to estimate; list leakage causes: unconfirmed prices, verbal add-ons, late sub costs, delayed final invoices.
5. Review deposits (liabilities), WIP, receivables age, cash flow around insurance stages, warranty reserve versus callbacks.
6. Review KPIs (`../templates/weekly-kpi-dashboard.md`): response time, days to estimate, close rate, callbacks, safety.
7. GATE (human): owner reviews the report and approves price book changes, write-offs and actions.
8. Update the price book and estimate defaults; log the changes.
9. Send tax and bookkeeping items to `../../09-business-admin-finance-compliance/README.md`.

## Outputs
Monthly margin report, action list, price book updates.
