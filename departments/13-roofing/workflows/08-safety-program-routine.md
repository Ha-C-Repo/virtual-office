# 08 Safety Program Routine

Owner: safety-officer | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Daily
1. Fall protection plan reviewed and signed for the site (`../templates/fall-protection-plan.md`).
2. Toolbox talk with attendance (`../templates/toolbox-talk.md`).
3. Harness fit, anchors, lifelines, ladders and guardrails inspected; damaged gear removed.
4. Weather, heat and power-line check.

## Weekly
5. Site spot check by the safety officer or a competent person; photos.
6. Review near misses and hazards found; assign corrections.

## Monthly
7. Review training records, certificates and expiry dates ({{TRAINING_PLAN}}); schedule refreshers.
8. Review the written safety program and hazard communication program for changes in work or rules (VERIFY against current OSHA text).

## Incident response
9. Stop work, protect people, get care. Notify the owner the same day.
10. File `../templates/incident-report.md`; determine root cause and corrective actions. Reportability and recordkeeping thresholds are checked against current rules (VERIFY).
11. GATE (human): owner reviews and approves corrective actions and any filings.

## Outputs
Signed plans, talk records, inspection logs, training log, incident files.

Skill: `../skills/fall-protection-plan/SKILL.md`.
