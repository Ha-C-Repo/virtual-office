# 09 Closeout to Final Payment and Lien Waiver

Owner: finance-controller with quality-inspector | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Final QC passed (`../skills/qc-checklists/SKILL.md`). Build and close the punch list (`../skills/punch-list-and-closeout/SKILL.md`, `../templates/punch-list.md`).
2. Customer walk-through with before and after photos.
3. GATE (human): owner approves the completion certificate (`../templates/completion-certificate.md`).
4. Confirm: permit final, change orders billed, cleanup done, leftovers reconciled.
5. Final invoice out promptly.
6. Exchange lien waivers: conditional final with the invoice, unconditional final after funds clear (`../skills/lien-waiver-tracker/SKILL.md`, `../templates/lien-waiver-tracker.md`; statutory forms VERIFY).
7. Insured jobs: recoverable-depreciation release request with completion docs, photos, final invoice and carrier forms, before the policy deadline (VERIFY).
8. Collections cadence for unpaid balances ({{COLLECTIONS_CADENCE}}); owner approves any payment plan or write-off.
9. Sub final payments after punch closed and sub waivers in (`../skills/subcontractor-vetting-and-payments/SKILL.md`).
10. Move the deposit from liability to revenue as earned; roll up the job cost (`14-monthly-job-costing-review.md`).
11. Start warranty registration (`10-warranty-and-service-call.md`) and the review request (`../templates/review-request-message.md`).

## Outputs
Completion certificate, final invoice, waivers, payment record, job cost closeout.
