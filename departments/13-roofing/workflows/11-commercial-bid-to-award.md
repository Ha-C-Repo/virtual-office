# 11 Commercial Bid to Award

Owner: commercial-bid-manager | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Receive the bid documents and addenda; build the requirements matrix (`../skills/commercial-bid-response/SKILL.md`).
2. GATE (human): bid or no-bid decision by the owner.
3. Pre-bid walk with photos and measurements; fall protection for the visit (`../skills/fall-protection-plan/SKILL.md`).
4. Moisture survey if indicated (`../skills/roof-moisture-survey-planner/SKILL.md`); verify by probes or cores.
5. Select the system among TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen and coatings, with manufacturer constraints (VERIFY).
6. Takeoff and pricing; margin check with `../harnesses/job_cost.py`.
7. Complete `../templates/commercial-bid-response.md`: experience, certifications, substitution controls, warranty term and type, exclusions, alternates, unit prices.
8. GATE: manufacturer-approved applicator status confirmed; safety plan reviewed.
9. GATE (human): owner signs the bid.
10. Submit; handle clarification questions; track the result.
11. On award: contract review, submittals, pre-installation meeting, permit, schedule, then `05` and `07`.
12. Record the result and reasons for loss or win.

## Outputs
Requirements matrix, bid package, award record.
