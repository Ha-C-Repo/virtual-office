# 04 Insurance Claim Support (End to End)

Owner: insurance-claims-coordinator | Approver: {{OWNER}}; the homeowner authorizes all carrier contact

## Steps
1. Homeowner decides to file a claim. Explain roles in writing (`../skills/insurance-claim-support/SKILL.md`). The office does not adjust or negotiate coverage.
2. Open the claim file with `../templates/claim-file-cover-sheet.md`: dates, deadlines to VERIFY from the policy and state rules.
3. Document damage (`../skills/damage-documentation-photo-log/SKILL.md`) and prepare the line-item estimate.
4. Adjuster meeting with the homeowner's consent (`../templates/adjuster-meeting-checklist.md`).
5. Receive the carrier scope. Compare line by line (`../skills/scope-comparison-and-supplement/SKILL.md`).
6. GATE (human): owner approves any supplement before it is sent; the homeowner authorizes submission.
7. Track payments with `../templates/acv-rcv-tracker.md` and `../harnesses/claim_math.py`: initial ACV payment, supplements, recoverable depreciation.
8. GATE: contract and any message pass `../skills/contract-compliance-check/SKILL.md` and `../skills/deductible-and-inducement-gate/SKILL.md`.
9. Handle mortgagee-endorsed checks: plan for delay and tell the homeowner what to expect.
10. Perform the work (`05`, `07`). Hidden decking and code items are photographed and submitted as supplements with evidence.
11. After completion, send the recoverable-depreciation release request with the completion package, before the policy deadline (VERIFY).
12. Disputes (matching, appraisal): record facts and refer the homeowner to the policy process and qualified representatives.
13. Close the claim file with the final accounting; hand to `09-closeout-to-final-payment.md`.

## Outputs
Claim file, scope comparison, supplement letters, tracker, release package.
