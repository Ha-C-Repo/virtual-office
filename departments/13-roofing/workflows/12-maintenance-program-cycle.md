# 12 Maintenance Program Cycle

Owner: commercial-bid-manager with service-warranty-coordinator | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Propose and sign the agreement (`../templates/annual-maintenance-agreement.md`; `../skills/maintenance-program-builder/SKILL.md`).
2. Calendar spring and fall visits plus post-storm visits.
3. Before each visit: fall protection plan, access, permits, notify the building contact.
4. Inspect; record findings with photos (`../templates/maintenance-inspection-report.md`).
5. Send the report with recommended repairs and priorities.
6. GATE: owner authorization from the customer before non-emergency repairs; record it.
7. Perform repairs; photos before and after; log in the warranty record.
8. Emergency calls follow the agreement terms and `10-warranty-and-service-call.md`.
9. Annual review: condition trend, remaining life estimate (illustrative, VERIFY), recommendation, renewal.
10. GATE (human): owner approves the renewal and any price change.

## Outputs
Visit schedule, inspection reports, authorization log, annual summary.
