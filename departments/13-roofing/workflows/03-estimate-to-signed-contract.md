# 03 Estimate to Signed Contract

Owner: sales-estimator | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Customer selects a tier. Record the selection and the color confirmation plan.
2. Prepare the contract from `../templates/residential-contract.md` with scope, price and payment schedule from the approved estimate.
3. GATE: `../skills/contract-compliance-check/SKILL.md` passes, including cancellation notice, insurance clause, and no deductible language beyond the homeowner's responsibility.
4. GATE: `../skills/deductible-and-inducement-gate/SKILL.md` passes.
5. GATE (human): owner signs off before the contract goes to the customer.
6. Send for signature. Deliver the cancellation notice at signing where required and record the date and time.
7. Collect the deposit per contract and state limits; book it as a liability until earned.
8. Signed copy to the customer and the project file; start the permit (`05-permit-to-dryin-to-final.md`) and the material plan (`06-material-order-to-delivery.md`).
9. Changes after signing use `../templates/change-order.md`: written, priced, signed before work.
10. Lost jobs: record `lost_reason` for the monthly review.

## Outputs
Signed contract, deposit record, cancellation notice record, project file opened.
