# 06 Material Order to Delivery

Owner: production-manager | Approver: {{OWNER}} above {{PO_APPROVAL_THRESHOLD}}

## Steps
1. Confirm verified measurements and the takeoff (`../skills/roof-takeoff-calculator/SKILL.md`). Re-run the harness.
2. GATE: customer confirms product and color in writing.
3. Confirm waste and coverage with the supplier (VERIFY).
4. Build the order (`../skills/material-order-builder/SKILL.md`, `../templates/material-order.md`).
5. GATE (human): owner approves orders above the threshold and any substitution.
6. Place the order; set the delivery window to match the weather window and crew availability.
7. Plan roof loading and dumpster placement; confirm access.
8. Delivery: count, check lot and color, photograph, note damage; mark `delivery_confirmed_at`.
9. GATE: delivery confirmation releases crew scheduling (`07-production-day-and-weather.md`).
10. After the job: reconcile leftovers, process returns, record credit.

## Outputs
Purchase order, delivery record, return record.
