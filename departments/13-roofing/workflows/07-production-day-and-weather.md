# 07 Production Day Routine with Weather

Owner: production-manager | Approver: {{OWNER}} for schedule changes affecting deadlines

## Steps
1. Evening before: check the forecast, delivery and permit status; run `../templates/job-kickoff-checklist.md`; issue `../templates/crew-work-order.md` (`../skills/crew-scheduling-weather/SKILL.md`).
2. GATE: signed fall protection plan on file; subcontractor COI valid.
3. Morning: confirm the weather call. If rain is expected in the work window, reschedule or limit the scope to what can be dried in.
4. Toolbox talk and equipment check (`../skills/fall-protection-plan/SKILL.md`, `../templates/toolbox-talk.md`).
5. Work proceeds; supervisor checks nail placement and alignment during the day, more often for piecework crews.
6. Mid-day photo checkpoint: deck, underlayment, flashing.
7. End of day: dry-in whatever is open, secure tarps and materials, magnet sweep, photos, record squares installed and issues.
8. Weather delay: notify the customer with an approved message, re-sequence the backlog (open roofs first), log the delay.
9. Daily log to the project file: hours, squares, crew, problems, safety observations.
10. Milestone pay requests go through `../skills/subcontractor-vetting-and-payments/SKILL.md` only with evidence.

## Outputs
Daily log, photos, schedule updates, delay record.
