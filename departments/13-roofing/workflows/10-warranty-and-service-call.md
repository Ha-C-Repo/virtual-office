# 10 Warranty Registration and Service Call

Owner: service-warranty-coordinator | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Registration
1. At completion identify the warranty layers (`../skills/warranty-registration-and-tracker/SKILL.md`).
2. Confirm installer certification for the tier sold.
3. Register with the manufacturer within the window, commonly about 30 days (VERIFY) using `../templates/warranty-registration-record.md`.
4. GATE (human): owner approves the workmanship certificate (`../templates/workmanship-warranty-certificate.md`).
5. Deliver the certificate and care/maintenance guidance to the customer.

## Service call
6. Receive the call; classify emergency, urgent, routine (`../skills/service-call-leak-triage/SKILL.md`).
7. Emergency: authorize a tarp in writing (`../templates/tarp-authorization.md`).
8. Create a ticket (`../templates/service-ticket.md`); visit; document cause with photos.
9. Decide covered, not covered or undetermined from the warranty form. GATE (human): owner decides goodwill.
10. Repair or quote; close the ticket; update the warranty log (inspections and repairs preserve validity).
11. Storm damage after completion goes to `04-insurance-claim-support.md`.
12. Quarterly: review callbacks by cause and crew; feed QC and training.

## Outputs
Registration record, certificate, tickets, warranty log, callback analysis.
