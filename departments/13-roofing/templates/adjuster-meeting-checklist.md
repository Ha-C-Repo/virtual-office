# Adjuster Meeting Checklist

Claim {{CLAIM_NUMBER}} | Property {{PROPERTY_ADDRESS}} | Date {{MEETING_DATE}} | Attendees {{ATTENDEES}}

The homeowner leads. Our role is to point out documented conditions and answer measurement questions. We do not argue coverage.

## Before
- [ ] Homeowner has agreed in writing that we attend
- [ ] Safe roof access planned (`../skills/fall-protection-plan/SKILL.md`)
- [ ] Photo set printed or on a device; test square locations marked
- [ ] Line-item estimate and measurement sheet
- [ ] Product data and manufacturer requirements relevant to the loss
- [ ] Prior repairs or maintenance history from the homeowner

## During
- [ ] Walk every slope and collateral damage together
- [ ] Show test squares and hit counts
- [ ] Note the adjuster's observations and any areas not inspected
- [ ] Ask what happens next and when; write down the answer
- [ ] Do not discuss the deductible beyond "the homeowner is responsible for it"

## After
- [ ] Notes filed in the claim file within one business day
- [ ] Follow-up for missing information with the homeowner's permission
- [ ] Scope comparison scheduled when the carrier scope arrives
