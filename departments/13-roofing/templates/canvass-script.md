# Canvass Script and Door Hanger (Compliant)

Use only after approval by {{OWNER}} and a pass of `../skills/review-and-referral-compliance/SKILL.md` and `../skills/deductible-and-inducement-gate/SKILL.md`. Approval date {{DATE}}.

## Before you knock
- [ ] Local solicitation permit or registration current ({{CITY_OR_COUNTY}}; VERIFY)
- [ ] Permitted hours only; photo ID and company shirt
- [ ] Skip any home with a do-not-solicit sign or on the internal list
- [ ] Carry the cancellation notice form for any off-premises sale (VERIFY)
- [ ] License {{LICENSE_NUMBER}} and insurance information available

## Opening
"Hi, I am {{REP_NAME}} with {{COMPANY}}, a local licensed and insured roofing contractor. We are in {{NEIGHBORHOOD}} after the recent {{EVENT_TYPE}}. We offer free roof inspections with photos. Is it a good time to talk for a minute?"

## If interested
"We can take photos from the ground and, if it is safe, check the roof. You will get a written summary and photos. There is no obligation. Which day works?"

## If they ask about insurance
"Your policy decides what is covered. If you decide to file a claim, you file it and you are responsible for the deductible. We can document the damage and give an itemized estimate. We do not negotiate coverage."

## Never say
- Anything about waiving, covering, rebating or absorbing a deductible, "free roof", "insurance pays for everything"
- That you represent the insurance company or can promise approval
- Fear claims without photos; pressure ("today only")
- Anything that gets around a no-soliciting sign
