# Glossary: Roofing

Terms are working definitions. Where a code or manufacturer instruction defines a term differently, the code or instruction controls.

## Geometry and measurement
- **Square**: 100 square feet of roof surface. Ordering squares (material, with waste) are tracked separately from tear-off and labor squares (surface actually removed or installed).
- **Pitch**: slope as rise per 12 units of run, written "6/12". Also called slope.
- **Pitch factor**: sqrt(rise^2 + run^2) / run. Multiplies footprint area to get sloped surface area. Computed by `../harnesses/takeoff_calc.py`.
- **Footprint**: plan-view area of the roof, including overhangs.
- **Waste factor**: extra material for cuts, starter, hips, valleys and mistakes. Varies with complexity (VERIFY with supplier).
- **Complexity**: count of planes, valleys, hips, dormers, penetrations and steep sections that drives waste and labor.
- **Aerial measurement report**: remote measurement product derived from imagery. Must be verified on site for complex roofs.
- **Test square**: a 10 ft by 10 ft (100 sq ft) area on a slope used to count hail or wind hits for an inspection.
- **Layers**: number of existing roofing layers. Many codes allow a maximum of two layers of asphalt shingle (VERIFY local amendment).

## Roof parts
- **Eave**: lower horizontal edge of a sloped roof.
- **Rake**: sloped edge at the gable end.
- **Ridge**: highest horizontal line where two planes meet.
- **Hip**: sloped external intersection of two planes.
- **Valley**: sloped internal intersection of two planes; carries concentrated water.
- **Gable / hip roof**: roof shapes; hip roofs usually need more waste.
- **Dormer, skylight, chimney, pipe boot, vent**: penetrations that need flashing.
- **Fascia, soffit, gutter, downspout**: edge and drainage components.
- **Decking / sheathing**: structural panels or boards under the roofing. Condition is verified at tear-off.
- **Rafter, truss**: structural framing members.

## Components
- **Underlayment**: water-resistant layer over the deck. Types and lap requirements follow code and manufacturer (VERIFY).
- **Ice barrier** (ice and water membrane): self-adhering membrane at eaves and valleys where required by code.
- **Starter strip**: first course along eaves and rakes.
- **Ridge cap**: shingles or accessories covering the ridge and hips.
- **Drip edge**: metal edge flashing at eaves and rakes.
- **Flashing**: metal or membrane that directs water away at joints and penetrations.
- **Step flashing**: stepped pieces where a roof meets a vertical wall.
- **Kickout (diverter) flashing**: directs water from a roof-wall joint into the gutter.
- **Counterflashing, coping, parapet, scupper, drain, curb**: commercial and low-slope components. A parapet is a short wall above the roof edge; coping caps it; a scupper is a drainage opening through it.
- **Ventilation / net free area (NFA)**: intake and exhaust opening area, balanced to the attic floor area (VERIFY code edition).
- **Bundle**: packaged shingles. Bundles per square vary by product (commonly 3 to 5).

## Process terms
- **Tear-off**: removing existing roofing to the deck.
- **Overlay (re-cover)**: installing new roofing over existing. Restricted by code and manufacturer.
- **Dry-in**: deck covered with underlayment and flashing so the structure is weather-resistant before shingles.
- **Punch list**: remaining small items before final acceptance.
- **Change order**: written, signed change to scope or price.
- **Lien waiver**: document releasing lien rights for a stated payment (partial or final). Statutory forms in some states (VERIFY).
- **Holdback**: portion of a payment withheld until a milestone is met.
- **Supplement**: request to the carrier for items or amounts missing from the first scope.

## Insurance terms
- **Adjuster**: carrier or independent representative who evaluates the claim. A contractor is not an adjuster unless licensed as one.
- **Scope**: list of work and quantities used to price the loss. Compared line by line to the contractor estimate.
- **RCV**: replacement cost value.
- **ACV**: actual cash value = RCV minus depreciation.
- **Depreciation**: value reduction for age and condition.
- **Recoverable depreciation**: depreciation paid back after the work is completed and documented, subject to policy deadlines.
- **Deductible**: homeowner share of the loss. Never waived, rebated or absorbed by the contractor (see `rules.md`).
- **O&P**: overhead and profit. Sometimes allowed on multi-trade claims (VERIFY).
- **Proof of loss**: sworn statement of the loss when the policy requires one; deadlines vary.
- **Appraisal clause**: policy process to resolve amount-of-loss disputes.
- **Matching**: dispute over replacing undamaged areas to achieve uniform appearance.
- **AOB**: assignment of benefits. Restricted in some states (VERIFY).
- **Mortgagee**: lender named on the policy; checks may require endorsement and cause delay.
- **Public adjuster**: licensed person who represents the policyholder on a claim.

## Commercial and warranty terms
- **TPO**: thermoplastic polyolefin single-ply membrane.
- **PVC**: polyvinyl chloride single-ply membrane.
- **EPDM**: ethylene propylene diene monomer rubber membrane.
- **Mod-bit**: modified bitumen, multi-ply or cap sheet membrane.
- **Built-up roof (BUR)**: layered bitumen and reinforcing plies.
- **Coating**: fluid-applied restoration layer over an existing roof.
- **Ballast**: stone or pavers holding a loose-laid membrane.
- **Membrane**: the waterproofing sheet or layer of a low-slope system.
- **Substrate / cover board / insulation**: layers beneath the membrane.
- **NDL warranty**: no-dollar-limit warranty; covers repair of leaks from covered defects without a cap, for a stated term and conditions.
- **Prorated warranty**: coverage that declines over time.
- **Workmanship warranty**: installer promise on labor and installation.
- **Material warranty**: manufacturer promise on product defects.
- **System warranty**: manufacturer warranty on a complete assembly installed by a certified applicator.
- **Moisture survey**: infrared, nuclear, capacitance or drone-based scan to locate wet insulation, verified by probes or cores. See ASTM C1153 for the infrared method (VERIFY current edition).

## Business and safety terms
- **PPE, PFAS**: personal protective equipment; personal fall arrest system.
- **Guardrail, safety net, warning line**: fall protection options.
- **Toolbox talk**: short scheduled safety briefing.
- **Competent person**: someone able to identify hazards and authorized to correct them.
- **COI**: certificate of insurance.
- **Job cost**: all direct costs charged to one roof.
- **Gross margin / markup**: margin = profit / price; markup = profit / cost. They are not interchangeable.
- **WIP**: work in progress schedule comparing billing to cost incurred.
- **Speed-to-lead**: elapsed time from inquiry to first human response.
