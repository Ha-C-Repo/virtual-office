# Fall Protection Plan and Toolbox Talks

## When to use
Before any crew goes on a roof, and every work day for the toolbox talk.

## Inputs
Roof type, pitch, height, edges, skylights and openings, power lines, access points, crew list and training, equipment on hand, the current regulation text (VERIFY).

## Plan steps
1. Choose the method for each roof area. For residential roofs at 4 ft or more above a lower level, the options are guardrail systems, safety nets or personal fall arrest systems. Use another method only when those are shown infeasible or create a greater hazard, then a written site-specific plan, a competent person and training apply (VERIFY the current OSHA text and any state plan).
2. Identify anchor points: location, rating, how installed and removed without damaging the new roof; rope grab, lifeline length, self-retracting lifeline use, harness size.
3. Plan ladder access: secured, extending about 3 ft above the landing, inspected.
4. List hazards: skylights, edges, wet or frosty surfaces, power lines, debris chutes, material staging, hot work on membranes.
5. Name the competent person on site and the stop-work authority statement.
6. Complete `../../templates/fall-protection-plan.md`; walk it with the crew lead; sign.

## Daily steps
1. Toolbox talk (about 5 to 10 minutes) with a topic: harness fit, anchors, ladders, heat illness, power lines, nail guns, housekeeping, hazard communication. Use `../../templates/toolbox-talk.md` and rotate topics with {{TOPIC_ROTATION}}.
2. Record attendees and signatures.
3. Inspect harnesses, lanyards, lifelines and anchors before use; remove damaged equipment.
4. Incidents and near misses: `../../templates/incident-report.md` the same day.

## Outputs
Signed plan, toolbox talk record, equipment inspection record, training log entries.

## Gates
- No plan, no roof. No signed talk, no start.
- Safety officer reviews the plan; owner is informed of any infeasibility claim.
- Do not copy statistics or fine amounts from blogs; cite regulation text.
- Training placeholders ({{TRAINING_PLAN}}): OSHA 10 style courses, manufacturer entry training, fall protection, first aid; retention is under-documented, so set targets locally.
