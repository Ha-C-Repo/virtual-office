# Roof Inspection Protocol

## When to use
Every sales inspection, claim inspection, warranty visit and pre-bid walk of a steep-slope roof.

## Inputs
Lead record, weather, access plan, inspector name, camera or phone with timestamps and location on, ladder, drone if used.

## Steps
1. **Decide access** before leaving: on-roof, ladder-only, drone, or ground. Do not go on a wet, icy, steep or visibly damaged roof. OSHA text treats inspection or assessment differently from construction work; treat that relief as narrow and **VERIFY locally** (`../../knowledge/rules.md`). If unsure, use fall protection or stay off.
2. **Ground first:** photograph all four elevations, gutters, downspouts, soffit, fascia, siding, window screens, vents, fences, AC fins and soft metals for hail marks.
3. **Roof overview:** pitch, number of layers (check at the edge), roof shape, complexity, ventilation, penetrations, skylights, chimney, valleys, flashing.
4. **Test squares:** mark a 10 ft by 10 ft area on each damaged slope. Count and record hits per square using the template. Do not apply an assumed threshold; record the count and let the carrier or office policy decide.
5. **Indicators:** hail (bruising, mat fracture, granule loss, collateral damage), wind (creased, lifted or missing tabs, debris), age (curling, cracking, granule loss), mechanical or installation defects. Say what is observed, not the cause, unless clear.
6. **Soft spots and decking:** note spongy areas from the surface, attic staining and daylight if accessible. Decking condition is confirmed at tear-off; mark as "to be verified".
7. **Photo protocol:** follow `../damage-documentation-photo-log/SKILL.md`.
8. Record safety concerns and any immediate leak risk; arrange a tarp if needed (`../service-call-leak-triage/SKILL.md`).
9. Summarize findings and a recommended path (repair, replace, monitor) in `../../templates/inspection-report.md`. Use plain language and attach photos.

## Outputs
Inspection report, photo log, test square table, measurement request, recommendation.

## Gates
- No scare tactics; findings must be visible in photos.
- Inspector does not state coverage or claim outcomes.
- Owner or senior estimator reviews the report before it goes to the customer.
- Photo-based or AI damage assessments are drafts only; a person verifies (`../../knowledge/rules.md`).
- Do not walk the roof without footwear, fall protection plan when required, and a second person aware.
