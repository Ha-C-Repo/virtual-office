# Roof Moisture Survey Planner

## When to use
Commercial low-slope roofs with suspected wet insulation, before recover, repair or replacement decisions.

## Inputs
Roof plan, age and system, leak history, access, weather window, survey method options, pilot qualification if a drone is used.

## Steps
1. Choose the method: infrared scan (ground, roof-top or drone), capacitance or nuclear gauge, or probes. Infrared surveys depend on a day with solar heating followed by a clear evening; method standards such as ASTM C1153 apply to infrared (VERIFY the current edition).
2. Drone operations: certified pilot, airspace and property permission, flight plan and weather limits, record keeping (VERIFY current aviation rules). If these are missing, do not fly.
3. Define the grid and mark anomalies on a roof plan with identifiers.
4. **Verify** anomalies: take probes or core samples at selected anomalies and at control areas. Photograph cores with layer description and moisture reading.
5. Map confirmed wet area by color: confirmed, suspected, dry. Compute areas.
6. Scope: remove and replace wet insulation, repair or recover where dry; quantify with a unit price for added wet areas found during work.
7. Report with `../../templates/maintenance-inspection-report.md` format or a survey section in the bid.

## Outputs
Survey plan, anomaly map, verification log, scope recommendation.

## Gates
- Infrared images alone are not a diagnosis; verification is required before final scope.
- Roof-top access follows `../fall-protection-plan/SKILL.md`, including edge and skylight protection.
- Owner approves survey cost and any drone flight.
