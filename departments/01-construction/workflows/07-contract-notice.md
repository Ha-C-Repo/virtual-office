# 07 Contract Notice

Owner: contract-administrator. Sign-off: {{APPROVER_ROLE}}. DRAFT ONLY until signed.

1. **Trigger.** A correspondence-register tag (DELAY, CHANGED_CONDITION, DIRECTION) or a project-controls variance, or weather beyond the contract threshold.
2. **Classify** the event: delay, changed condition, constructive change, variation.
3. **Pull clause and period** from the obligations register. If unknown: NEEDS_INPUT, stop, ask. Never guess a period.
4. **Compute the last day** to give notice; put it on the calendar with a warning at half the period.
5. **Collect evidence:** diary entries, emails, photos, delivery records.
6. **Draft** from `../templates/contract-notice.md`. Facts only.
7. **HUMAN SIGN-OFF.** The principal or counsel reviews anything that may become a claim.
8. **Send** by the contractually required method; log it with proof of delivery.
9. Follow up in 5 business days; record the response.
