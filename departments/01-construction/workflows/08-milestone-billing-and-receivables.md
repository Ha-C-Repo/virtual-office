# 08 Milestone Billing and Receivables

Owner: contract-administrator. Hand-off to business admin and finance (09) for ledger entries.

1. **Milestones:** submittal deposit; percentage at signing; percentage on material arrival; percentage on schedule-of-values progress. Terms follow the contract; the quote validity window is stated on the bid.
2. **Trigger** each milestone from a verified event (signature date, receiving record, approved percent complete).
3. **Draft invoice** (`../templates/milestone-invoice.md`) or pay application (`../templates/pay-application-seed.md`). Percent complete comes from approved diary data.
4. **HUMAN SIGN-OFF:** {{APPROVER_ROLE}} approves and sends.
5. **Track** status: draft, sent, paid, overdue.
6. **Reminders** at 7, 14 and 21 days overdue (drafts for approver).
7. **Interest:** compute statutory late-payment interest with the calculator.
8. **Lien calendar:** note preliminary notice and filing windows.
9. **At 21 days** or a pattern of late payment, escalate to the principal and consider notice or stop-work provisions per contract.
10. Weekly receivables aging to the principal.
