# 02 Four-Pass Review (before any output)

Owner: bid-reviewer.

1. **Calculation cross-check.** Two independent paths. Stop if divergence is over 10 percent: re-take-off, do not footnote.
2. **Drawing-reading completeness.** Confirm every structural sheet was viewed, general notes read, conflicts resolved or escalated, every fact traces to a sheet.
3. **Internal-information leak scan.** Supplier names, individual names, margins, headcount, internal rates, cash-flow rationale, superseded terms.
4. **Layout and rendering scan.** Text overflow, clipped tables, orphaned headers, real logo present, nothing shrunk to fit.

GATE: any failed pass returns the work to the producing agent. No partial release.

After delivery, send the principal a one-line summary: what went out, headline number, confidence tier, open flags.

Report what you observed, not what you expect. A clean pass over LOW-confidence input is still a LOW-confidence deliverable.
