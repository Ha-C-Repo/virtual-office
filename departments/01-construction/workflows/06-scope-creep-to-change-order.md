# 06 Scope Creep to Change Order

Owner: contract-administrator. Sign-off: {{APPROVER_ROLE}}.

1. **Detect.** Scan inbound email, RFI responses, meeting minutes and revised drawings for trigger phrases ("can you also", "not in the drawings", "client now wants", "additional work", "design change").
2. **Qualify.** Scope creep or clarification? If unclear, ask the requester or the principal. Check the always-out-of-scope list.
3. **Document in writing before work.** Log in the correspondence register. If a client-caused delay is involved, start `07-contract-notice.md` in parallel.
4. **Estimate.** Engineering hours, shop hours, tons times blended hours, overhead and profit multiplier at locked rates. Calculator only.
5. **Draft** (`../templates/change-order.md`), number `CO-{{DATE}}-{{SEQ}}`, state schedule days.
6. **HUMAN SIGN-OFF:** {{APPROVER_ROLE}} signs.
7. **Submit** to the client. Status SUBMITTED. Follow up at 7 days.
8. **Track:** ACCEPTED or REJECTED. Margin tracked separately from the base contract.
9. GATE: no work proceeds unsigned. If the client insists on direction to proceed, escalate to the principal and issue a notice (07).
