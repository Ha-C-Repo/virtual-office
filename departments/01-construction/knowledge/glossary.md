# Construction Glossary

**Estimating**
- Takeoff: measured quantities from drawings. BOQ: quantities with labor, plant and subcontract context (hours). BOM: materials only. Pricing schedule: structure of priced lines. ROM: rough order of magnitude estimate.
- IFC / CD / DD / SD: drawing completeness stages (issued for construction, construction documents, design development, schematic or budget). Each maps to a risk adder and contingency.
- Item classes: COL, BEAM, JST (joist), DECK, PLATE, ANCH (anchor), MISC. Modes: COUNT (each), LINEAR (feet), AREA (square feet).
- Intensity: weight per square foot. Price per ton, per square foot. Rate band: p25, p75, floor, ceiling.
- Gross area confidence: HIGH (stated or GC-confirmed), MED (measured, single building), LOW (assumed or multi-building without per-building areas).
- CSI MasterFormat divisions; deck sections 05 31 (steel deck) and 05 36 (composite deck).

**Parties and documents**
- GC: general contractor. EOR: engineer of record. SOQ: statement of qualifications. VE: value engineering. SOV: schedule of values. Pay app: payment application. Change order. RFI: request for information. NCR: nonconformance report. RIR: receiving inspection report. MTR: material test report.
- GP report: internal gross-profit report, confidential twin of the proposal.
- PEMB: pre-engineered metal building. Submittals. Shop drawings. Connection detail.

**Contract risks**
- LD: liquidated damages. Pay-if-paid. Retention. No-damages-for-delay. Broad-form indemnity. Notice time bar.
- Prompt-pay interest: statutory late-payment interest. Prevailing-wage rules for public work. Domestic-material requirement.

**Shop and quality**
- Heat number, lot number, piece mark, traveler. CWI: certified welding inspector. NDT, VT, UT, MT: nondestructive and visual testing methods. WPS: welding procedure specification. DFT: dry film thickness. IAS-type special inspection. SJI: joist standards body. AISC: structural steel standards body. AWS D1.1: structural welding code. ASTM A992, A500, A36, F1554 with specified minimum yield (Fy) and tensile (Fu). CE: carbon equivalent.

**Safety**
- EMR / experience mod, TRIR, DART, OSHA 300A: safety metrics and forms.

**Governance**
- Verify-do-not-generate. Confidence tag. Claim object (value, confidence, source). Facts manifest. Rate lock. Live document (read at source, never mirrored). Skeleton protection. Tier 1 (immutable compliance), Tier 2 and 3 (owned by named roles).
- Two-document rule: client document plus internal `-GP` twin.
- Turnaround window: planned plant shutdown period in industrial outreach.
