# Construction Schemas (field names only)

**Takeoff row:** item_id, designation, mode, qty, unit, primary_source, secondary_source, confidence, sheet, bbox, notes, lb_per_ft, weight_lb, tons, formula_ref. Note tokens: CONFLICT, GROUP, OPENING, QTY_BASIS, REMOVED.

**BOQ row:** boq_id, source_item_id, assembly_id, stream (MATERIAL / LABOR / EQUIPMENT / SUBCONTRACT), description, qty, unit, hours.

**Assembly:** assembly_id, description, provenance, applies_to{item_class, mode, designation_includes, designation_excludes}, openings, streams{materials, labor_hours, equipment_time, subcontract}, notes.

**Trade pack manifest:** pack_id, display_name, pack_version, masterformat_divisions, modes, units, controlling_inputs[input_id, description, sourcing, low_confidence_policy], material_authority, gates_module, measured_classes. Item class: class_id, description, modes, primary_source_keyword, measured, authority_resolvable, pricing_line. Scope checklist: base, additions{group: items}, by_building_type{base, add_groups, add_items}. Benchmarks: intensity_metric, price_metric, bands_by_building_type, convergence_check{methods, max_spread_pct}.

**Company profile:** profile_id, legal_name, enabled_packs, governance{rate_lock_authority, assembly_change_approver, tier2_owner, tier3_owner}, brand{logo_light, logo_dark, rules, tokens}, locale{units, region}. **Proposal rules:** documents{client_proposal, gp_report_suffix}, hard_checks, always_in_scope, fold_into_rates, standard_exclusions, positioning_line.

**Tender index:** doc_id, filename, class, pages, needs_ocr, page_text[page, text]. **Requirement register:** req_id, category, discipline, text, expected_unit, source_refs, priced_line_ref. **Inclusions/exclusions:** list_id, category, discipline, text, derived_from_req_ids. **Estimate line:** line_id, requirement_refs, category, qty, unit, unit_rate, extended, rate_basis. **Reconciliation:** status (Gap / Orphan / ok), issues, verdict. **Contract risk:** risk_type, severity, clause_ref. **Production-rate library:** item_class, p25, p75, floor, ceiling, last_updated.

**Project:** name, client, gc_contact, contract_sum, signed_date, sqft, est_tons, milestones_paid, material_arrived, sov_pct_complete, notes. **Invoice:** invoice_number, project_name, client, date, due_date, milestone, amount, status, sent_date, paid_date, reminder_count. **Purchase order:** po_number, project_name, vendor, date, line_items[description, quantity, unit, unit_price, total], subtotal, status. **Change order:** co_number, project_name, date, trigger_text, description, scope_basis, est_hours, est_cost, schedule_days, status, notes.

**Reference tables:** shape database (type, designation, weight_per_foot, area, depth, web thickness, flange width, flange thickness, moment of inertia, section modulus, radius of gyration); cost factors (category, bare material unit, labor hours per ton, labor rate, equipment per ton); labor rates (trade, county, base, fringe, total, comp class, source); permit fees (jurisdiction, fee type, base, per-sf rate, threshold, notes); inspection rates; insurance rates; risk config (risk id, description, probability, impact pct); T&M tasks (task id, description, median hours, unit, rate, material per unit).

**Site diary:** RAW_MESSAGES(msg_id, timestamp, source, chat_name_or_user, sender, body, media_link, processed); DIARY(date, project, supervisor, weather, work_summary, safety_notes, photos_link, approved, approved_ts); LABOR(date, project, employee, hours, cost_code); QUANTITIES(date, project, cost_code, qty, unit); DELIVERIES(date, project, supplier_internal, item, docket_link); DELAYS(date, project, type, hours_lost, notes); TASKS(created, project, task, owner, due, status, source_msg_id); COST_CODES(code, activity, unit, budget_qty, budget_hours).

**Shop QC:** projects(id, code, job_number, name, contract_number, tonnage, ias_required, status); bol_items(project_id, line_number, section, quantity_ordered, quantity_received, heat_number, lot_number, astm_grade, fy, fu, ce, mtr_verified, received_date); pieces(project_id, piece_id, section, heat_number, lot_number, status, label_printed, traveler_type); traveler_fields(piece_pk, field_number, field_name, value, signed_by, timestamp, notes); ncrs(project_id, piece_pk, gate, category, description, opened_by, opened_date, disposition, disposition_authority, eor_reference, closed_by, closed_date, status); rir_records(project_id, lot_number, signed_by, signed_date, all_checks); weld_records(piece_pk, weld_type, wps_file, welder_id, pre_weld_by, pre_weld_date, vt_result, vt_by, vt_date, ndt_type, ndt_result, ndt_report_ref); release_records(piece_pk, shop_director_sign, cwi_sign, executive_sign, release_date, truck_load_ref, certificate_printed); fastener_lots(project_id, assembly_type, quantity, lot_no, markings_verified, mfr_cert_on_file, galvanized, lube_check_done, received_complete, received_date); audit_log(table_name, sync_uid, op, payload, source, station, applied_at, outcome). Sync tables add sync_uid and updated_at.

**Other:** supplier quote log (material, quantity, unit, unit_price_internal, lead_time, expiration, reference, logged_date); RFI log (number, spec_section, status, due); AR milestone record; injury-rate inputs (hours_worked, recordable_cases, days_away_cases); scheduled task (id, label, hour, minute, days, enabled, description).

**Document numbering:** {{PREFIX}}-{{YEAR}}-{{PROJECT_ABBREV}}-{{SEQ}}. Suffixes: `-GP` internal report, `-ALT` alternate, `-SOQ`; PEMB infix; revision increment.
