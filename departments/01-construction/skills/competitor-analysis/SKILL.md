---
name: competitor-analysis
description: Free-source competitor research for two segments (core trade, adjacent services) producing a bid, pass, reprice or differentiate recommendation.
---
## When to use
Before pursuing a new segment or when win rate drops.

## Inputs
Segment, region, competitor list from public sources.

## Steps
1. Use free public sources only. Respect site rules; attribute sources.
2. Build a data table: competitor, services, size indicators, public projects, positioning.
3. Summarize positioning advantages and gaps.
4. List items to verify (everything from scraped or inferred sources is LOW).
5. Recommend: bid, pass, reprice or differentiate.

## Outputs
Summary, data table, advantages, verify list, recommendation.

## Gates
No private data, no pricing guesses presented as fact.
