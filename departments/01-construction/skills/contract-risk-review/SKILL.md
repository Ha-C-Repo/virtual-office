---
name: contract-risk-review
description: Scores contract clauses against eleven risk types and produces a severity-ranked contract risk register.
---
## When to use
On receipt of conditions of contract or a subcontract, before bid release and again before signing.

## Inputs
Conditions of contract, subcontract, addenda.

## Steps
1. Scan for the eleven risk types: pay-if-paid or pay-when-paid, retention, liquidated damages, no-damages-for-delay, broad-form indemnity, consequential damages, termination for convenience, flow-down terms, warranty duration, change-order limitations, notice and claim time bars.
2. Record clause reference, risk type, severity (Critical, High, Medium, Low) and suggested response (accept, clarify by RFI, price, exclude, escalate).
3. Add notice periods to the obligations register (`../../templates/obligations-register.md`).
4. Mark Critical and High items for the principal.

## Outputs
Contract risk register (`../../templates/contract-risk-register.md`).

## Gates
Critical items are escalated before the bid is released. This is a risk screen, not legal advice; counsel reviews the executed contract.
