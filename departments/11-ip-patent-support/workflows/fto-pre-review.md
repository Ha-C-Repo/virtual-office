# Workflow: Freedom-to-Operate Pre-Review

1. Trigger: pilot, launch, or major design change planned.
2. Engineering supplies the feature list and target markets.
3. Analyst runs `../skills/freedom-to-operate-prereview/SKILL.md`.
4. Coordinator reviews and packages for counsel.
5. **Gate:** counsel gives the opinion. Launch decisions wait for it.
6. Engineering reviews design-around options with the principal.
7. Record the outcome; set a re-review date.
