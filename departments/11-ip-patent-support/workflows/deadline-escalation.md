# Workflow: Deadline Escalation

Trigger: a deadline is inside the first warning window, or inside 14 days without counsel acknowledgment.

1. Send the coordinator the row: asset, deadline, type, source document, date recorded.
2. Coordinator sends a counsel briefing (`../templates/counsel-briefing.md`) asking: is the deadline correct, who acts, and by when.
3. No reply in 2 business days: second message, copy the principal.
4. No reply in 5 business days or deadline inside 7 days: principal contacts counsel directly.
5. Record the outcome from a document. If none arrives, record "outcome unknown" and keep the item open.
6. Missed deadline: preserve all records, stop discussion of cause in shared channels, and send to counsel the same day.

**Gate:** nothing is filed, extended or abandoned by the office. Counsel and the principal decide.
