# Workflow: Trademark Clearance

1. Candidate names collected; shortlist of no more than {{MAX_CANDIDATES}}.
2. Worksheet per candidate (`../templates/trademark-clearance-worksheet.md`).
3. Searches run and logged (`../skills/trademark-clearance-steps/SKILL.md`).
4. Send worksheet to counsel. **Gate:** counsel opinion required.
5. **Gate (sign-off):** principal selects the name.
6. Choose basis and classes; counsel files.
7. Track examination and statement-of-use deadlines on the calendar.
8. After registration, record the details in the IP register and set renewal reminders.
