# Workflow: Invention to Counsel

1. Engineer or founder reports an invention to the coordinator.
2. Capture `../templates/invention-disclosure.md`. Confirm assignment is signed.
3. Freeze disclosure: no public talks or posts on this topic until counsel advises.
4. Prior-art workflow (`../skills/prior-art-workflow/SKILL.md`).
5. Strategy prep (`../skills/patent-strategy-prep/SKILL.md`).
6. Briefing to counsel (`../templates/counsel-briefing.md`). **Gate (sign-off):** principal approves.
7. Counsel advises on filing. Record decision and deadlines.
8. Update the disclosure calendar and the IP register.
