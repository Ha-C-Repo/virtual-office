# Rules: IP Support

1. This department prepares and organizes. Licensed counsel advises, opines and files.
2. Verify, do not generate: cite only documents opened and read. Never invent identifiers, dates or citations.
3. Describe, do not conclude: never say "infringes", "is patentable", "is cleared" or "is protected".
4. Public disclosure can affect rights and deadlines in many jurisdictions; treat any public disclosure as a counsel question before it happens.
5. Privileged material stays in the counsel folder and is not forwarded.
6. NDAs and assignments come before sharing and before contributions.
7. Every external IP statement (to investors, partners, customers) is approved by the principal and, for IP status, by counsel.
8. Keep an IP register: assets, status, deadlines, owner entity.
9. Third-party licenses are tracked for all included components.
10. Deadlines are calendared with early reminders; missed deadlines are escalated immediately.
