# Glossary: IP Support

- **Claim hierarchy:** broad independent concept, narrower dependent features, fallback positions.
- **Novelty argument:** explanation of how the invention differs from known approaches.
- **Prior art:** publicly available information relevant to an invention.
- **FTO (freedom to operate):** analysis of whether a product may infringe others' in-force rights.
- **Provisional filing:** an initial, lower-formality patent filing that secures an early date; counsel decides use.
- **Conception evidence:** dated records showing when and by whom an idea was formed.
- **Assignment:** written transfer of invention rights to the company.
- **Privilege:** legal protection for confidential communications with counsel; can be lost by sharing.
- **Waiver:** loss of privilege or confidentiality through disclosure.
- **NDA:** non-disclosure agreement.
- **Clearance:** counsel's opinion on a mark's availability; searching alone is not clearance.
- **Filing basis:** use in commerce or intent to use.
- **Classes:** categories of goods and services in trademark filings.
- **Examination / office action:** the registry's review and its objections.
- **Statement of use:** proof of actual use filed after an intent-to-use application is accepted.
- **Specimen:** evidence of the mark as used.
- **Design lock / ADR:** recorded architecture decision; helps date conception.
- **IP register:** the single sourced list of IP assets, status and deadlines.
- **SPDX identifier:** short standard id for a software license (for example MIT, Apache-2.0).
- **Permissive / copyleft / source-available:** triage labels for license families; not legal conclusions.
- **Trade secret:** information kept secret and protected by reasonable measures, with value from secrecy.
- **Assignment gap:** a contributor with no signed assignment, or one signed after work began.
- **Docketing:** tracking legal deadlines with warnings.
