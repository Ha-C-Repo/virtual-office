# 11 - IP and Patent Support

## Purpose
Support a startup's intellectual property work between the business and outside counsel: organize invention disclosures, prepare patent strategy inputs, run prior-art and freedom-to-operate pre-reviews, manage trademark clearance steps, protect confidentiality, and keep communications with counsel and investors consistent. This department prepares and organizes. It does not give legal advice and does not file anything.

## Scope note
The source material for this department is thin (skill outlines only). The department is therefore intentionally small: 3 agents, 7 skills, 6 templates, 4 workflows.

## When to use
- A new invention or product feature may be patentable.
- Before public disclosure, investor sharing or a pilot, to check IP exposure.
- Before choosing, using or filing a product or company name.
- Preparing materials for patent or trademark counsel.
- Checking whether a planned product could infringe others' rights.

## Hard rule
Every legal conclusion, filing decision, opinion and filing itself belongs to licensed counsel. Output from this department is labeled "prepared for counsel review".

## Agents (`agents/`)
- `ip-coordinator` - lead; routing, privilege, counsel communications
- `patent-support-analyst` - disclosure intake, claim hierarchy draft, prior-art workflow, FTO pre-review
- `trademark-support-analyst` - six-step clearance process support

## Skills (`skills/`)
patent-strategy-prep, prior-art-workflow, freedom-to-operate-prereview, trademark-clearance-steps, ip-protection-hygiene, counsel-communications, investor-ip-disclosure

## Templates (`templates/`)
