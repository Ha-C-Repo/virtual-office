# Freedom-to-Operate Pre-Review

## When to use
Before launch of a pilot or commercial product, or before a significant design change.

## Steps
1. List the product features and the markets where it will be made, sold and used.
2. Search for in-force patents touching the key features; log in the prior-art log with a status column (in force, expired, unknown).
3. For each in-force candidate, summarize the independent claim in plain words and map which product features appear to overlap, without concluding infringement.
4. Prioritize by closeness and by the owner's activity.
5. Hand to counsel for an opinion. Note design-around ideas for engineering to consider.

## Gates
Only counsel gives an FTO opinion. Findings are confidential and kept in the counsel folder.
