# Investor IP Disclosure

## When to use
Preparing a one-pager or an 8-10 slide deck, or answering IP diligence questions.

## Steps
1. Decide the level of detail: what the technology does and why it matters, not how it works internally, until an NDA is signed.
2. Describe IP status accurately: "patent application prepared with counsel" only if true; do not say "patented" or "protected" without counsel confirming.
3. Mark documents with the confidentiality legend.
4. Confirm an NDA is in place before technical slides.
5. Follow-up email: thanks, what was shared, next step; no new technical detail.
6. Log what was shared, with whom and when in the disclosure log.

Gate: the principal and counsel approve any statement about IP status.
