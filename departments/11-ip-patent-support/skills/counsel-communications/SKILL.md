# Counsel Communications

## Steps
1. One topic per message. State the decision you need from counsel.
2. Attach: disclosure, prior-art log, worksheet, timeline of disclosures.
3. Separate facts (with dates and sources) from your questions and from your opinions.
4. List deadlines.
5. Use `../../templates/counsel-briefing.md`.
6. Mark "Privileged and confidential: prepared for counsel".
7. File the reply in the counsel folder; log decisions.

Gate: the principal approves anything sent to counsel that commits budget.
