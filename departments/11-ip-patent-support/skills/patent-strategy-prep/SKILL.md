# Patent Strategy Prep

## When to use
After a disclosure is captured and before counsel meets about filing.

## Inputs
Completed `../../templates/invention-disclosure.md`, prior-art log, product roadmap, disclosure calendar.

## Steps
1. Restate the invention in one sentence: problem, key technical step, advantage.
2. Draft claim hierarchy: the broadest concept first, then dependent claims adding features, then fallback positions (`../../templates/claim-hierarchy-draft.md`).
3. Draft the novelty argument: what known approaches do, what this does differently, supported by the prior-art log. Label as "for counsel".
4. Note timing facts: conception date, first public disclosure, planned launches, investor meetings.
5. Questions for counsel: provisional versus full filing, jurisdictions, budget.

## Gates
Counsel decides what to file and when. The principal approves budget.
