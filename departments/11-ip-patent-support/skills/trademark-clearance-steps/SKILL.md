# Trademark Clearance Steps

1. **Clearance.** Search registers, business names, domains and common-law use for identical or confusingly similar marks. Record in the worksheet. Counsel gives the opinion.
2. **Filing basis.** Already in use in commerce, or intent to use. Record first-use dates and specimens if in use.
3. **Classes.** Choose the goods and services classes that match the real and planned business; avoid claiming goods you will not offer.
4. **Filing.** Counsel (or the principal with counsel review) files. Keep copies and serial numbers in the file.
5. **Examination.** Track office actions and response deadlines; draft factual input for counsel.
6. **Statement of use.** If filed on intent to use, calendar the deadline; gather specimens of actual use.
