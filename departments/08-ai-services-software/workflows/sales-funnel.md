# Workflow: Sales Funnel

Owner: `sales-engineer`.

1. **Lead in.** Check ICP tier. Selective-tier leads get a lighter touch.
2. **Outreach** (cold email: specific subject, one observation, one credibility line, one call to action, about 120 words). **Gate:** principal approves outbound copy and lists.
3. **Discovery (60 min)** with `../templates/discovery-script.md`. Create the customer config.
4. **Demo (30 min)**, pain-first, one flow (`../skills/discovery-and-demo/SKILL.md`).
5. **Async video recap** (about 5 minutes, script for approval).
6. **Trial** per `../templates/trial-agreement-outline.md`. No auto-convert.
7. **Mid-trial check** at the halfway point; usage review.
8. **Tier selection.** Proposals above threshold get customer and internal margin versions. **Gate:** principal approves price and terms.
9. **Close or lose.** Win: onboarding (`customer-onboarding-config.md`). Loss: win/loss note, reason, competitor.
