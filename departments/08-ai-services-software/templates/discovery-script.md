# Discovery Script (60 minutes): {{PROSPECT_COMPANY}}

## Pre-call prep (10 minutes)
Public information on the business, vertical, size, likely tools. Hypothesis of the top pain: {{HYPOTHESIS}}.

## Opening (5)
Purpose, agenda, time check, permission to take notes.

## Their business (10)
- How does work flow from lead to paid?
- Where does it break most often?

## Current stack (10)
- What do you use today for scheduling, quoting, invoicing, time?
- What do you like and dislike?

## Pain (10)
- What does that cost you in hours or lost jobs? (use their numbers)
- Why now?

## Buying context (10)
- Who else is involved? How are decisions made? Budget range? Timeline?

## Qualification (3)
Fit with ICP tier {{ICP_TIER}}: yes / selective / no.
