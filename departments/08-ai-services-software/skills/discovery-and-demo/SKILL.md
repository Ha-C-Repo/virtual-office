# Discovery and Demo

## When to use
Any qualified lead booking a call.

## Discovery (60 minutes)
Follow `../../templates/discovery-script.md`: pre-call research, opening, their business, current tools, buying context (who decides, budget, timeline), qualification, close with a clear next step. Record answers directly into the customer config.

## Demo (30 minutes)
1. Recap the top pain in their words.
2. Show one flow that solves it, using their vertical config terms and realistic sample data.
3. Show the customer-facing portal if relevant.
4. Close to a free trial. Offer an async video recap.

## Rules
- Pain first, features second. One flow beats a tour.
- Never invent customers, case studies or metrics. If there is no proof yet, say so.
- Trials do not auto-convert; data is retained for a stated period, then deleted.

## Outputs
Updated config, demo brief, follow-up video script or email for approval.
