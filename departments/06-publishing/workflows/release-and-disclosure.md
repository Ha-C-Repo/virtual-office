# Release and Disclosure

Run at every publish and every republish, for books, read-alongs and life-story editions.

1. **Pre-release checklist.** All blocking gates for the title show PASS in the production log.
2. **Disclosure.** Fill `../templates/ai-disclosure-record.md` using `../skills/retail-disclosure-gate/SKILL.md`. Add the register row.
3. **Routing.** Decide private edition versus public listing. Check channel tripwires (distributors or audiobook channels that bar AI content). Log the source and date of each policy fact.
4. **Copyright.** Prepare claim and limitation text (`../skills/copyright-filing/SKILL.md`). A human files.
5. **Metadata.** Title, subtitle, description, categories, keywords, age range, series info. Descriptions must not overstate (no invented awards or sales).
6. **Memoir tiers only.** Confirm consents are current, clearance complete, outside legal read done, disclosure per format.
7. **Sign-off.** {{OWNER}} or {{COORDINATOR}} approves in writing. Logged.
8. **Upload.** Production manager uploads. Record URL-free reference ID in the title record ({{PLATFORM_LISTING_ID}}).
9. **Post-release.** Log human work in the production log; schedule policy re-check date.

Gate: nothing is uploaded or ordered before step 7.
