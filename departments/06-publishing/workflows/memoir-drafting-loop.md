# Memoir Drafting Loop (recurring, per chapter)

1. **Back up** the current chapter file with a timestamped name describing its purpose. Never overwrite.
2. **Brief.** Complete `../templates/memoir-chapter-brief.md` from the timeline and interview notes.
3. **Draft** from the brief only (content guard: only brief content words and exact numbers).
4. **Quote check.** Run the quote-verifier script against the source archive. Any quote not found verbatim is removed.
5. **Ledger.** Update the passage authorship ledger (tier 1, 2 or 3; default down when unsure).
6. **Measure.** Voice fidelity and slop baseline (`../skills/memoir-anti-slop/SKILL.md`). Thresholds are fixed beforehand.
7. **Assertion audit.** Every claim diffed to source (`../skills/memoir-drafting-and-assertion-audit/SKILL.md`).
8. **Human pass.** About eleven spoken-aloud questions per chapter, then the subject reads and responds.
9. **Rewrite rule.** A passage that fails twice is handed to the subject to write.
10. **Changelog line.** Date, chapter, change, backup file.

Gate: chapter is accepted only when steps 4 to 8 are clean and the subject approves in writing (logged).
