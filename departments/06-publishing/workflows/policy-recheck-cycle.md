# Policy Re-check Cycle (about quarterly)

1. List every rule in the platform policy register: platform, rule, source, date checked, re-check date.
2. For each rule due, re-read the primary source (the platform's current published policy). Do not rely on summaries or memory.
3. Update date checked and set the next re-check date.
4. If a rule changed, flag affected titles and open the disclosure record for each.
5. Items you cannot verify go to the open-items list (item, status). They are not acted on where money or publication is involved.
6. Summarize changes for {{OWNER}}. Owner decides on republish.

Gate: no republish triggered by a policy change without owner approval.
