# Picture Book Skeleton (ages 3 to 5 and 5 to 8)

- Title: {{BOOK_TITLE}}
- Age band: {{AGE_BAND}}

- Spreads: {{SPREAD_COUNT}} (commonly 12 to 16)

## Story shape
1. Opening: character, place, want. ({{OPENING}})
2. Problem or wish arises. ({{PROBLEM}})
3. Three attempts, each escalating or varying. ({{ATTEMPT_1}}, {{ATTEMPT_2}}, {{ATTEMPT_3}})
4. Turn: the character acts on a strength shown earlier. ({{TURN}})
5. Resolution, shown not stated. ({{RESOLUTION}})
6. Final image echoes the first. ({{ECHO}})

## Age notes
- 3 to 5: simple sentences, strong refrain, concrete emotions, short spreads.
- 5 to 8: more varied sentences, light subplot, some humor that rewards rereading.

## Rules
- Every right page ends with a hook.
- Art carries information the text omits.
- No stated moral.
