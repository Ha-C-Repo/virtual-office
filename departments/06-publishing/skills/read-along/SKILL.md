# Read-Along

## When to use
When a title ships with narration, in ebook or video form.

## Inputs
Final approved text, spread timings, narration voice choice, rights documentation.

## Steps
1. Decide narration source: a human narrator, or synthesized voice. Record which in the AI disclosure record.
2. If synthesized: confirm the voice is licensed or owned, not a clone of a real person without written consent. Log the license in `../../templates/permissions-log.md`.
3. Narrate at a pace suited to the age band. Pause on page turns. Keep character voices distinct but gentle.
4. Sync text highlighting or page turns to narration. Verify timing on the final export.
5. Listen through the whole file with a human. Check mispronunciations, breaths, clicks, level changes.
6. For video versions, hand off to `07-ai-video-creative-production`.

## Quality bar
Warm, unhurried, clear consonants, no robotic cadence at sentence ends, consistent loudness, no music that masks words.

## Gates
- Marketplaces for audiobooks may bar synthesized narration. Check the current rule before routing.
- Human listen-through sign-off is blocking.
