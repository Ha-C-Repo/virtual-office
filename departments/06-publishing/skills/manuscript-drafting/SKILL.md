# Manuscript Drafting

## When to use
Pipeline step 2 (`../../workflows/book-pipeline.md`), after the concept record is approved.

## Inputs
- Approved `../../templates/concept-record.md` (age band, theme, spread count, series fit).
- Series bible if the title belongs to a series (character truth lives there only).
- Brand voice and kill-list from `../../knowledge/rules.md`.
- The matching skeleton: `../../templates/board-book-skeleton.md`, `../../templates/picture-book-skeleton.md` or `../../templates/early-reader-and-chapter-book-skeleton.md`.

## Steps
1. Pick the age band and read its budget (words per spread, spreads, sentence shape) from the skeleton.
2. Fill `../../templates/spread-map.md` first: spread number, pages, beat, illustration note, right-page hook. Draft text only after the map is complete.
3. Write text spread by spread. Each spread carries one beat. Never write continuous prose and cut it afterwards.
4. End every right-hand page with a hook: a question, a reveal-in-waiting, a repeated refrain with a twist.
5. Keep refrains identical word for word where the skeleton calls for repetition.
6. Mark any rhyme for the meter gate (`../rhyme-meter-gate/SKILL.md`).
7. Save as numbered working file 02. Back up before each revision.

## Outputs
- Completed spread map with verbatim text.
- Draft word count per spread and total versus budget.

## Gates
- Over budget means cut the draft, not the budget.
- Do not proceed to art direction until readability (`../readability-gate/SKILL.md`) and the human read-aloud both PASS.
- A human writes or rewrites the final text. The draft is a proposal.
