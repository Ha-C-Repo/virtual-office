# Memoir Interview Protocol

## When to use
Pipeline steps 10 and 11 (gate G5b).

## Gap analysis
1. Build a matrix of life domains (for example family, home, school, work, health, relationships, faith, travel, money, community) against eras (about ten life periods).
2. Mark each cell: well covered, thin, empty. Cite the timeline events behind it.
3. For each thin cell write candidate questions tagged by purpose class (clarify a date, resolve a conflict between sources, fill a gap, capture a feeling, identify people, confirm spelling, recover a lost story, and similar). Add a risk_flag where the topic is sensitive.
4. Order the questions with {{OWNER}} or the human interviewer; remove any that would lead.

## Interview rules
- A human leads the interview. The assistant prepares packs and takes notes, nothing more.
- Ask open questions. Do not propose ages, dates, names, colors, weather or emotions.
- No confirmatory reinforcement. Do not repeat a detail back as if confirmed unless the subject said it.
- Respect sensitive-category preferences; offer to skip.
- Record with consent only. Keep notes in the subject's words.
- Mark recounted stories (told to the subject by others) as such; they are not first-hand.
- Watch for false-memory amplification: if the subject is unsure, record uncertainty rather than resolving it.

## Outputs
Interview pack per session, session notes with quotes, updated timeline v2 inputs, and a list of unresolved questions.

## Gates
Subject reviews the notes before they are used. G5b sign-off by the human interviewer.
