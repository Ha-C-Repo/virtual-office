# Quality Bar

## When to use
After the readability gate and before the human read-aloud, and again before release.

## Twelve failure modes (any hit means revise)
1. Generic, interchangeable plot with no specific detail.
2. Moral stated outright at the end instead of shown.
3. Filler adjectives and stacked adjectives.
4. Repeated sentence openings with no purpose.
5. Rhyme forced by bent word order.
6. Inconsistent character look or name across spreads.
7. Expression that contradicts the text.
8. Text baked into images or garbled lettering.
9. Hands, faces or objects with visible generation artifacts.
10. Pacing without a hook on right-hand pages.
11. Vocabulary above the age band without support.
12. Copy that reads as template output (same shape across titles).

## Six credibility differentiators (aim for all)
1. A real author presence with a real bio.
2. Credited art direction.
3. Low release volume with visible care per title.
4. A specific, personal premise.
5. Honest AI disclosure on the page where required.
6. Consistent series bible visible to the reader.

## Gatekeeper tells
Retailers and reviewers look for: batches of near-identical titles, stock-like covers, mismatched metadata, no author presence. Avoid the pattern, not just the symptom.

## Steps
Score the title against the list, log hits and fixes, and have a human confirm.
