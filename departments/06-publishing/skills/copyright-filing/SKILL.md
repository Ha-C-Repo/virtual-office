# Copyright Filing

## When to use
Pipeline step 14, after the disclosure record is complete. Escalate legal questions to counsel.

## Principles (verify against the current office guidance and log date)
- Human authorship is required for protection. Material generated by AI without sufficient human creative control is generally not protectable and must be disclaimed.
- Protectable human contributions can include: human-written text, the original selection, coordination and arrangement of AI and non-AI material (compilation), and human modifications that are themselves creative.
- Registration requires disclosing AI-generated content that is more than minimal and excluding it from the claim.

## Steps
1. Using the title's production log, list human-authored elements and their evidence.
2. List AI-generated elements (for example, individual illustrations as generated).
3. Draft the claim text: human-authored text and selection and arrangement of text and images. Draft the Limitation of Claim text excluding AI-generated material.
4. Fill `../../knowledge/schemas.md` AI disclosure register fields `limitation_of_claim_text` and `copyright_status`.
5. A human reviews and files. Record the filing date and reference in the register.

## Business consequences to note for the owner
- AI-generated illustrations may be copied by others without recourse.
- Strong production logs are the evidence that human authorship exists.
- Series names and characters may be better protected by trademark; hand off to `11-ip-patent-support`.

## Gates
Never state a registration is granted until the certificate is in hand. Counsel review for anything contested.
