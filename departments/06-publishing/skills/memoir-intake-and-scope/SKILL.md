# Memoir Intake and Scope

## When to use
Pipeline step 1 (gate G1) for every new life-story engagement, before any data is received.

## Inputs
Inquiry from {{SUBJECT}} or a requester, the proposed deliverable tier, intended audience.

## Steps
1. **Decline screens (A1 to A6).** Decline or refer out if any apply. Typical screens: the requester is not the subject and has no documented authority; the subject lacks capacity or consent cannot be established; the aim is to harm, expose or defame a named living person; the subject is a minor; the project requires professional advice outside scope (legal, medical, therapeutic); material was obtained unlawfully. Record which screen fired.
2. **Sensitive-category preview.** Tell the subject up front that these topics may appear and are handled with extra care: health, sexuality, religion, immigration status, criminal history, finances, minors, deceased relatives, abuse. Record the subject's preferences (include, restrict, exclude).
3. **Third parties.** Explain that other people appear in memoirs and that naming living people triggers clearance (see `../persons-and-clearance/SKILL.md`).
4. **Message-thread notice.** If message exports will be provided, warn that they contain other people's words and are used only as reference.
5. **Four scope selections.** Record: deliverable tier (T0 timeline, T1 story map, T2 drafted manuscript, T3 private family edition, T4 public release); authorship mode (A Organizer, B Collaborator, C Draft-first); pseudonymization level (P0 none, P1 names changed, P2 names and identifying details changed, P3 composite); data sources to be used.
6. **AI-use disclosure to the subject.** State plainly how AI is used (parsing, drafting support), what is not done (no detail originates with the system), and that raw data stays local.
7. Record the outcome in `../../templates/memoir-deliverable-agreement.md`.

## Outputs
Gate G1 record: PASS, DECLINE (with reason) or REFER.

## Gates
Human sign-off by {{OWNER}} before consent documents are issued. No data is requested before G1 PASS.
