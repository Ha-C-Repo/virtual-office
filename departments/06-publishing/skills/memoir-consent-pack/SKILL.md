# Memoir Consent Pack

## When to use
Pipeline step 2 (gate G2), and any time scope or processing changes.

## Required documents (4)
1. Services and authorship agreement (deliverable tier, authorship mode, ownership, fees as {{FEE_SCHEDULE}}).
2. Data processing consent (what data, where stored, local-only processing, retention, destruction).
3. AI-use disclosure acknowledgment.
4. Sensitive-category consent (per category: include, restrict, exclude).

## Documents needed when applicable (4)
5. Third-party release or notice, where a named person agrees to appear.
6. Voice or recording consent for interviews.
7. Permission for any licensed material the subject supplies (photos, letters, artwork).
8. Publication consent for public-release tiers.

## Non-document preconditions
- Identity and authority of the signer verified by a human.
- The subject has requested their own data exports directly from sources (the office does not log in to the subject's accounts).
- Storage location and retention date set.

## Ongoing consent
- Consent is revisable. Re-confirm at each tier change, before any interview recording, before clearance, and before publication.
- Record the date of the latest confirmation. If consent lapses or is withdrawn, stop processing, seal data, and escalate to {{OWNER}}.

## Gates
G2 requires all required documents executed and filed. A human checks signatures. No ingestion starts earlier.
