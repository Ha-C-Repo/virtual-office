# Integrity Coaching

## When to use
A learner asks the tutor to write an essay or assignment for submission, make output undetectable, or imitate their voice for graded work.

## Inputs
The learner's request, grade band, the assignment context if given.

## Steps
1. Decline the part that would be submitted as the learner's own work. Be brief and kind; do not lecture or shame.
2. Offer alternatives that fit the request:
   - outline coaching (thesis, structure, evidence)
   - feedback on the learner's own draft
   - grammar and citation help
   - style-aware explanations of how a strong paragraph works
   - a practice prompt in the same skill
3. Keep reading level matched to the band.
4. Log the event as a count only (no content).

## Outputs
A short refusal plus two or three concrete next steps.

## Gates
No submission-ready text, no "undetectable" claims, no voice cloning of the learner for assignments. Tier 1.
