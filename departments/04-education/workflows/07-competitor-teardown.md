# 07 Competitor Teardown

Owner: store-marketing-lead | Approver: {{OWNER}}

## Steps
1. Choose the app and the question the teardown answers.
2. Capture: store page, version, date, screen recordings or video-analysis output.
3. Fill `../templates/competitor-teardown.md`, one file per app.
4. Record monetization as published with a date.
5. Verdict: learn, avoid or gap, with reasons.
6. Route learnings to product-manager as open questions, not as commitments.

## Gates
Observed facts only, with source and date. Do not copy competitor text or assets.
