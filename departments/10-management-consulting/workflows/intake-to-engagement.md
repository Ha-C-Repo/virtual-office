# Workflow: Intake to Engagement

1. Prospect responds. Intake call (`../skills/intake-qualification/SKILL.md`, `../templates/intake-call-notes.md`).
2. Fit decision. **Gate:** principal approves taking the client.
3. Propose the fixed-fee 30-day plan with `../templates/engagement-letter-outline.md`. **Gate (sign-off):** principal and counsel approve terms before sending.
4. Signed letter received; set up an isolated client folder (no other client data).
5. Send `../templates/information-request-list.md`.
6. Kickoff: dates for interviews, decision session and memo.
7. Move to `diagnose-phase.md`.
