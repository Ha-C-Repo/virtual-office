# Workflow: Diagnose Phase (about 3 weeks)

1. Week 1: data in, issue tree (`../skills/issue-tree/SKILL.md`), work plan.
2. Week 1-2: interviews (`../skills/executive-interview/SKILL.md`), stakeholder map.
3. Week 2: analysis; test hypotheses; close branches; note gaps.
4. Week 3: draft memo (`../templates/diagnostic-memo.md`) with top 3 opportunities, top 3 risks, and the avoided decision.
5. Editor pass: structure and voice audit (`deliverable-editor`).
6. **Gate (sign-off):** engagement partner and principal approve.
7. Deliver memo to the owner privately first; schedule the decision session.
