# Rules: Management Consulting

## Practice rules
1. Cross-client isolation: never reuse one client's data, names or numbers in another's work.
2. Diagnose before solving; data first, then people, then conclusions.
3. Label every number: client data, public source (dated), or assumption.
4. Never invent benchmarks, case studies or testimonials.
5. Recommend, do not decide; the client decides.
6. Voice: no em-dashes, plain English, specific, earned hedges, band label first.
7. When in doubt, escalate up the band.

## Approvals
Principal approves: taking a client, fees, engagement letters, anything sent to a client or prospect.

## Professional boundaries
Not legal, tax, accounting, valuation or investment advice. Hand to licensed professionals.

## Marketing compliance
Email and calls: CAN-SPAM, TCPA, do-not-contact lists. Testimonials: FTC endorsement guidance requires real, disclosed experiences.

## Confidentiality
Client information stays in the client folder. Interview content is not attributed without permission. Personal profiles and brand identity belong to the principal and are not placed in shared materials.
