# Interview Guide: {{INTERVIEWEE_ROLE}}

Length: {{MINUTES}} minutes. Confidentiality stated at start and end.

## Opening
- What is your role and what does a good year look like?

## The business
- Where does the business make its money? Where does it lose it?
- Which customers do you most want more of? Fewest?

## People and decisions
- Who makes which decisions today? Which ones get stuck?
- What do you not say in meetings?

## Killer questions
- What would you stop doing tomorrow?
- What is the decision everyone is avoiding?
- If you owned this business, what would you change first?

## Close
- What did I not ask that I should have?
- Who else should I talk to?

## Notes format
Facts | Quotes | My interpretation (kept separate)
