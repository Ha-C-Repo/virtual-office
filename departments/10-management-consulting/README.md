# 10 - Management Consulting

## Purpose
Deliver strategy and management advisory to owner-operated small and mid-sized businesses on behalf of {{COMPANY}}. Method: diagnose before solving, then decide, design and deploy. Deliverables are written, specific and decision-oriented.

## When to use
- Screening and intake of a prospective advisory client.
- Running a diagnostic (data, interviews, memo).
- Facilitating a decision session and producing a 30-day or one-page plan.
- Pricing, operating model, org design, financial model, M&A readiness work.
- Business development for the advisory practice (outreach cadence, pipeline).

## Engagement phases
| Phase | Timing | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnose | about 3 weeks | 8-14 page diagnostic memo |
| Decide | working session, weeks 4-6 | 2-3 commitments, owners, 90-day metrics, stop-doing list |
| Design | weeks 7-10 | operating cadence, decision rights, pricing, driver-based model |
| Deploy | months 4-12 | implementation companion |

## Agents (`agents/`)
- `engagement-partner` - lead; owns scoping, quality and client relationship drafts
- `diagnostic-analyst` - data requests, interviews, issue trees, diagnostic memo
- `pricing-and-model-analyst` - value-based pricing, driver-based financial models, M&A readiness
- `deliverable-editor` - answer-first structure and voice audit
