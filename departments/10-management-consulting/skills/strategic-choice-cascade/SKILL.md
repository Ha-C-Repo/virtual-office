# Strategic Choice Cascade

## When to use
Decide phase, when the client must choose between paths.

## Steps
1. Answer in order: What is our winning aspiration? Where will we play? How will we win? What capabilities must we have? What management systems do we need?
2. For each of two to four options, list what would have to be true on customers, economics, capabilities and competitors.
3. Test each condition with data or a cheap experiment; rate confidence.
4. Choose the option with the fewest unlikely conditions. State the rejected options and why.
5. Run `../pre-mortem-and-inversion/SKILL.md` on the chosen path.

Five-forces and portfolio views may be used when they fit the question; do not apply them by default.
