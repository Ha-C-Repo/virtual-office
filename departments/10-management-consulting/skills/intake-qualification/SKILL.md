# Intake Qualification

## When to use
First conversation with a prospect.

## Target profile fields
Revenue band {{REVENUE_BAND}}; location radius {{RADIUS}}; owner-operated; real P&L; stress trigger: stalled growth, margin compression, succession, model fatigue, brand drift, founder burnout, family friction, post-acquisition, expansion uncertainty.
Verticals served: healthcare practices, professional services, manufacturing, real estate and development, technology/SaaS, retail/hospitality/consumer.

## Steps
1. Free short call. Use `../../templates/intake-call-notes.md`.
2. Check disqualifiers: no real P&L, owner will not share data, wants a pre-decided answer validated, cannot commit time, outside scope.
3. Identify the trigger and the avoided decision.
4. If fit: propose the fixed-fee 30-day plan, pay on satisfaction, optional implementation retainer (`../../templates/engagement-letter-outline.md`).
5. If not fit: decline kindly with a referral idea.

## Gates
Pricing and any commitment go to the principal (band WAIT or BRIEF).
