# Decision Session

## When to use
After the diagnostic memo has been read by the client.

## Steps
1. Pre-wire stakeholders (`../stakeholder-map/SKILL.md`).
2. Agenda: confirm the diagnosis (20 minutes), options and trade-offs (40), choose (30), assign (20).
3. Limit to two or three commitments. Each gets a named owner, a date and a 90-day metric.
4. Produce the stop-doing list: at least one item.
5. Write the decision memo within {{MEMO_HOURS}} hours (`../../templates/decision-memo.md`) and the 30-day plan (`../../templates/thirty-day-plan.md`).

## Gates
The client decides; the adviser recommends. Record dissent. Principal approves the memo before it is sent.
