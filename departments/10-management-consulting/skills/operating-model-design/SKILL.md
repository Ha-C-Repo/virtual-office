# Operating Model Design

## When to use
Design phase, or when growth has outpaced informal management.

## Steps
1. Capability map: what the business must be good at, rated current versus needed.
2. Decision-rights matrix (`../../templates/decision-rights-matrix.md`): for each recurring decision, who recommends, agrees, decides, performs, is informed.
3. Org: spans (reports per manager) and layers; remove layers before adding roles.
4. Cadence: weekly operating review, monthly performance review, quarterly planning, annual strategy; define inputs and outputs of each.
5. Talent and change: identify critical roles, succession gaps, and the change plan with sponsor and communication.
6. Pilot for 30-60 days, adjust.

Do not recommend hires or compensation figures without client data and the principal's review.
