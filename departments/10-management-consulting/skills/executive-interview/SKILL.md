# Executive Interview

## When to use
Diagnose phase; also for stakeholder alignment before a decision session.

## Steps
1. Use `../../templates/interview-guide.md`; tailor to the role.
2. Start with the person's role and what a good year looks like.
3. Ask the killer questions: What are you not saying in meetings? What would you stop doing tomorrow? What is the decision everyone is avoiding? If you owned this business, what would you change first?
4. Listen for contradictions with the data.
5. Take notes in facts, quotes, and your interpretation, separated.
6. Confirm confidentiality terms at the start and the end.

## Outputs
Interview notes feeding the stakeholder map and the memo.
