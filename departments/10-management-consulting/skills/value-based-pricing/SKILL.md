# Value-Based Pricing

## When to use
Margin compression, discounting, new offers, or price increases.

## Steps
1. Economic value to the customer: reference value (next best alternative) plus differentiation value, minus what they must give up. Mark each input as client data, public, or assumption.
2. Price sensitivity: run a short customer survey (too cheap, bargain, getting expensive, too expensive) or conjoint if volume allows; estimate elasticity from history where available.
3. Pocket-price waterfall: list price to realized price after discounts, rebates, freight, terms; find leakage.
4. Price-pack architecture: good, better, best; fences between tiers; offer ladder (one-time package, monthly retainer, per-item).
5. Recommendation with test plan and floor price.

## Gates
Pricing recommendations are BRIEF at minimum. Never state competitor prices without a dated public source.
