# Pre-mortem and Inversion

## Steps
1. Assume it is {{DATE_PLUS_12_MONTHS}} and the plan failed. List the reasons, silently first, then together.
2. Cluster the reasons; rank by likelihood and damage.
3. Inversion: what would we do to guarantee failure? Check whether we are doing any of it now.
4. For the top risks add a mitigation, an owner and an early warning metric.
5. Add the results to the decision memo and the 90-day metrics.
