# Outreach Cadence

## Steps
1. Qualify against the target profile (`../intake-qualification/SKILL.md`).
2. Touch plan: day 1, 4, 8, 14, 21, 30 and a final break-up note; vary channel (email, professional social, call).
3. Each message: specific subject, one observation about their business, one credibility line, one clear ask; about 120 words.
4. Skip the prospect if you cannot find a real observation.
5. Log touches in `../../templates/pipeline-tracker.md`.
6. Weekly pipeline review: stage, next step, age, drop stale deals.

## Pipeline stages
Identified, contacted, replied, intake call, proposal, signed, delivered. Required properties: stage, trigger, next step date, owner.

## Gates
Principal approves messaging and lists. Respect email and phone marketing rules (CAN-SPAM, TCPA); honor opt-outs immediately.
