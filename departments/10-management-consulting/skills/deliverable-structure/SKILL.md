# Deliverable Structure

## Steps
1. Write the answer in one or two sentences before anything else.
2. Frame: situation (what is true), complication (what changed or hurts), question (what must be decided).
3. Use action titles: each heading states the conclusion ("Margin fell because discounts rose") not the topic.
4. Support each conclusion with at most three points; check they are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive.
5. Ghost deck first: draft headings and the chart you expect, before building content.
6. Cut anything that does not support the answer.
7. End with decisions needed, owners and dates.
