# M&A Readiness

## When to use
Succession, sale, acquisition or post-acquisition engagements.

## Steps
1. Owner goals: timing, price expectations, role after sale, family and staff concerns.
2. Exit options: internal transfer, sale to a strategic buyer, sale to a financial buyer, wind-down.
3. Quality of earnings prep: normalize owner expenses, one-off items, revenue concentration, working capital; reconcile to tax statements with the client's accountant.
4. Value drivers to fix before going to market (customer concentration, key-person risk, documentation).
5. For acquisitions: choose an integration archetype (absorb, preserve, best of both, transform) and write the 100-day plan.
6. Hand to attorneys and accountants for valuation, structure and tax.

## Gates
No valuation opinions, legal or tax advice. All of these require licensed professionals.
