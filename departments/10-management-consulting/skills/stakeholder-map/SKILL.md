# Stakeholder Map

## When to use
Before a decision session or any change that affects roles, pay or ownership.

## Steps
1. List stakeholders, including family members, lenders, key customers and key employees.
2. Score influence and interest; record the stance on each option (supporter, neutral, blocker).
3. Note what each gains or loses and who they trust.
4. Plan the sequence: who to align before the session, who to hear last.
5. Apply the trust equation to your own plan: credibility, reliability and intimacy over self-orientation.
