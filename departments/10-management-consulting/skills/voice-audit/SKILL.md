# Voice Audit

## Modes
Internal; client-facing; logistics and follow-up.

## Hard rules
No em-dashes. Plain English. Specific over vague. Earn the hedge. Band label (AUTO, DRAFT, BRIEF, WAIT) as the first line of every output.

## Five passes
1. Dash sweep.
2. Banned vocabulary (filler such as "leverage", "robust", "delve", "game-changing").
3. Vague hedge ("somewhat", "perhaps", "it could be argued").
4. Specificity: replace general claims with numbers, names of roles, dates.
5. Length and structure: answer first, no rhetorical closers, no three-adjective stacks, no "not just X, it is Y".

## Common failures
Generic opener; stacked adjectives; false contrast; hedging everything; ending on a flourish.

## Output
Corrected text plus a log row (`../../templates/voice-audit-log.md`).
