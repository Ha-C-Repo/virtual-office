# Lead Follow-Up Workflow

Agent: retention and lead specialist. Skill: `../skills/lead-followup/SKILL.md`.

## Steps
0. Consent-register check per lead and per channel (`../skills/consent-register/SKILL.md`). No row, expired, revoked, wrong channel or low-confidence evidence: no draft; issue a named refusal stating what would cure it.
1. Choose cadence by channel.
   - Call: within 24 hours, day 3, day 7, then monthly; 8am to 9pm local; do-not-call honored.
   - Text: scoped consent only; maximum one per week; opt-out in every message.
   - Email: welcome within 24 hours, then maximum one per week; working unsubscribe.
2. Draft in principal voice; name consent basis and expiry in the `-INT` note.
3. Run the voice check and scrubber.
4. GATE: principal approval before anything is sent.
5. At 30 days, run the expiry sweep; expired consent stops all drafts.
6. Transfers: disclosure and prior written consent for data sharing; log in the correspondence register.
7. Opt-outs: update the register the same day.

## Sign-off
Principal approves each batch of drafts. The office never sends without a human.
