# Contract Lifecycle Workflow

Skills: `../skills/obligations-register/SKILL.md`, `../skills/correspondence-register/SKILL.md`, `../skills/contract-notice/SKILL.md`.

## Steps
1. Before signature: run the pre-signature sweep of the correspondence register for negotiated items missing from the final text. GATE: principal reviews the list.
2. Sign. File the signed copy.
3. Extract obligations to the register: deliverable, signature gate, money moment, notice.
4. Log correspondence with action required and response due.
5. Monthly sweep: overdue items flip to AT RISK; missed items get a NOTICE-CANDIDATE tag.
6. Draft the notice (`../templates/contract-notice.md`). Draft only.
7. GATE: principal approves; counsel reviews if it touches indemnity, termination, E&O or regulatory matters.
8. Sent by the principal. Log the sent notice in the correspondence register.
9. Renewal or non-renewal decision memo 90 days before term end.

## Sign-off
Principal at steps 1, 7 and 9.
