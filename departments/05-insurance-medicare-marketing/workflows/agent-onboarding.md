# Agent Onboarding Workflow

Owner: agent onboarding coordinator. Skill: `../skills/agent-onboarding/SKILL.md`.

## Steps
1. Receive the new-agent record. Confirm identity details are held in the agent folder only.
2. Create the nine-folder scaffold.
3. Instantiate the checklist from `../templates/onboarding-packet.md`: all twelve steps "not started", evidence "none".
4. Collect step evidence in order. Update the status only when a real document exists and its path is recorded.
5. Open carrier contracting rows (`../skills/carrier-contracting-tracker/SKILL.md`), one per carrier, running in parallel.
6. Track pre-licensing, exam, background check, license application, producer number, E&O, annual certification.
7. Mark ready-to-sell per carrier per plan year only with the carrier's notice on file.
8. Plan non-resident licenses and the continuing-education calendar.
9. Draft the welcome sequence in principal voice.
10. Assemble the onboarding packet and its `-INT` memo; run `outward-deliverable-chain.md` for anything sent to the agent.

## Gates and sign-off
- Gate: no step is complete without evidence.
- Sign-off: principal approves the welcome sequence and the packet before delivery.
- Weekly: operator reviews the checklist; stalled steps are listed in the internal memo.
