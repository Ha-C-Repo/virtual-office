# AEP Readiness Workflow

Skill: `../skills/aep-readiness/SKILL.md`.

## Steps
1. June: re-verify the regulator calendar and rule regime for the year.
2. Build the master calendar (training, certification, September 30 notice, October 1 marketing start, October 15 to December 7, January calls, open enrollment January 1 to March 31).
3. Pull the roster and contracting tracker. Build the per-agent checklist.
4. Marketing readiness: list the season's pieces, run each through `outward-deliverable-chain.md`, and submit plan-specific pieces to carriers early.
5. Produce the gap report (`../templates/aep-gap-report.md`): PASS, GAP, UNVERIFIED per item.
6. Chase GAP and UNVERIFIED items weekly.
7. Draft the kickoff email (`../templates/aep-kickoff-email.md`).
8. After September 30, confirm annual notices went out and run `retention-season.md`.
9. January: new-effective-date calls and post-AEP issue sweep.

## Gates and sign-off
Gate: no PASS without evidence. Sign-off: principal approves the kickoff email and the final readiness status before October 1.
