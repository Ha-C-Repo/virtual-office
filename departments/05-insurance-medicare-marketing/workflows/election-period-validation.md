# Election-Period Validation Workflow

Skill: `../skills/election-period-validator/SKILL.md`.

## Steps
1. Collect the client situation and the date of the proposed election. Use a masked ID.
2. List candidate periods and windows.
3. Check dual-eligible and low-income-subsidy rules.
4. Record window, citation and confidence for each.
5. Ambiguous cases route to the carrier or the federal help line. Never guess.
6. Return the table to the licensed agent, who confirms with the client.

## Gates
Re-verify rules every year. Open enrollment is not marketed as an extra enrollment opportunity. The AI never makes the enrollment decision.
