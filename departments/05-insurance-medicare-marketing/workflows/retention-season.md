# Retention Season Workflow

Skill: `../skills/client-retention/SKILL.md`.

## Steps
1. August: gather carrier notices of change as they arrive; cite each.
2. Build the plan-change list; cross with the book to list affected clients.
3. Verify the annual notice reached clients by September 30.
4. On or after October 1: draft outreach from `../templates/plan-change-client-letter.md`. No next-year plan talk before that date.
5. Consent check per channel before any call, text or email.
6. Run `outward-deliverable-chain.md` on each template version.
7. Tag switch-risk clients (plan on change list, unresolved service issues, rapid-disenrollment notices, returned mail) and respond with service first.
8. January: welcome calls for new effective dates.
9. February: post-AEP issue sweep, service not marketing.
10. Mid-year: renewal reviews. September: kickoff for the next cycle.

## Sign-off
Principal approves letter versions and the call script. Open enrollment is never promoted as an extra enrollment opportunity.
