# Rules (generic compliance and operating)

Re-verify every date and rule regime each year against the regulator. When a rule cannot be verified, flag it and route to a human; do not assume.

## Tier 1 hard rules
1. No consumer PHI or PII in outputs or stored artifacts outside approved systems. Sample records are labeled SAMPLE. No PHI, commission or production data into unapproved third-party AI tools.
2. Federal Medicare marketing rules apply to all Medicare outward content: required disclaimers verbatim, no prohibited claims, no scare tactics, no mischaracterization of "free".
3. TCPA and do-not-call: no outreach without documented consent or an existing business relationship. Name the consent status in every lead workflow.
4. No commission schedules, override splits or margins on external documents.
5. No unverifiable superlatives. Every statistic cites source and year.
6. Carrier names only per appointment and approval status. No carrier disparagement. No misleading side-by-side comparisons.
7. Never fabricate license numbers, producer numbers, appointments, certification status, enrollment figures or compliance status.
8. Logos and brand marks from approved masters only; never recreated. Trademark references descriptive only. Demonstration label on every outward artifact until the engagement is live.
9. E-signature and disclosure rules apply to anything signed.
10. PDF only for anything leaving the office.
11. No em-dashes; no filler language.
12. Never assert branch production, size or track record without principal confirmation.

## Universal checks for Medicare artifacts
Could it read as government-originated (seals, eagle, card imagery, "official" framing, envelope teasers)? Any implied government endorsement? "Medicare" in sender, headline, URL or email suggesting origin? "Free" used for a zero-dollar premium, cost-sharing reductions or subsidies? Superlatives without current or prior-year substantiation and source? Materially misleading benefit, network or cost statements? Plan-specific content is marketing and goes through the carrier for federal submission before use. Timing: no marketing of the next plan year before October 1; concurrent-year pieces state which year. No carrier confidential data.

## Third-party marketing organization disclaimer
Two variants: (a) does not sell every plan in the area, with the real organization count and plan count; (b) sells all plans. Leaving a placeholder is BLOCK. Required verbally before benefit discussion on sales calls, prominently on the website, in print and broadcast materials, and electronically on email and chat. Older wording included a state counseling reference; check the regime by date.

## By artifact type
- Print: disclaimer; no mimic-government teasers; nominal gifts must state no obligation.
- Events: declare as educational or sales. Educational events may not market plans, distribute applications or hold sales presentations; sign-in optional; no cherry-picking screenings; no meals, light snacks only; same-venue sequencing rules vary by regime.
- Calls: no cold calls; documented permission valid 12 months; disclaimer; full recording; SOA before a personal appointment; transfer disclosure.
- Email and mail: prior consent, working opt-out, disclaimer conveyed electronically, no door-to-door invitations.
- Web and social: disclaimer; lead-form disclosure that data is shared with a licensed agent and with whom; truthful testimonials.

## Registers and clocks
SOA valid 12 months from agreement, retained 10 years. Permission to contact valid 12 months. Call recordings retained per regime (newer regime minimum 6 years). No register row is deleted before retention end. Status changes append to notes.

## Enrollment periods
Annual enrollment October 15 to December 7. Medicare Advantage open enrollment January 1 to March 31, with a prohibition on knowingly marketing it as an extra enrollment opportunity (servicing and member-initiated inquiries allowed). Initial and special periods go through the election-period validator. Annual notice delivered by September 30.

## Compensation
The regulator publishes per-application fair-market-value maximums for MA and PDP by market each contract year. Renewal is capped at half of initial. Reconciliation flags any paid amount above cap.

## Other operating rules
Call hours 8am to 9pm local. STOP and unsubscribe honored the same day. Lead vendor consent documentation (language, date, source) on file before any dial. Verify the current one-to-one consent rule each season. Materials submission is via the carrier; a gate PASS never substitutes for carrier approval. Any consumer-facing AI voice or chat requires principal approval and disclosure.

## Document numbering
{TYPE}-{YYYY}-{ABBREV}-{NNN}, TYPE in REC, MKT, ONB, RPT, CMP. Internal memo appends -INT. Revisions increment NNN.

## Output formatting
Prose over bullets in reports. PDF final. Visual QC. Demonstration label. Unresolved placeholders (TBD, TBA, PENDING) fail; bracket placeholders warn in demo mode.

## Voice
Block: em or en dashes; three-adjective lists; "not just X, it is Y"; hedging; unresolved placeholders; page padding; filler openers; hollow clichés. Warn: long sentences; adjective-heavy text; jargon in client text; engineering as a line item.
