# Client retention

## When to use
September through January each year, and for mid-year renewal reviews.

## Inputs
Book of business, carrier annual notices of change (cited), consent register.

## Steps
1. Build the plan-change list from carrier notices, citing each.
2. Cross it with the book to produce the affected-client list.
3. Draft outreach from `../../templates/plan-change-client-letter.md`. Next-year plan talk only on or after October 1.
4. Consent check per channel before any call, text or email.
5. Annual cadence: January welcome calls; February post-AEP issue sweep (service, not marketing); mid-year renewal reviews; September kickoff.
6. Switch-risk signals: plan on the change list, unresolved service issues, rapid-disenrollment notices, returned mail. Response is service first.

## Outputs
Lists, letters, talking points, internal memo.

## Gates
Full path through `../../workflows/outward-deliverable-chain.md`. Open enrollment is never marketed as an extra enrollment opportunity.
