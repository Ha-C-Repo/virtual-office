# Voice check

## When to use
On every outward draft and every internal document. Stay silent unless there are violations.

## Personas
- Principal voice (recruits, agents, anything external): 10 to 18 words per sentence; warm, plain, confident; specific numbers; talks about agents' success and client security.
- Operator voice (internal operations): 12 to 20 words; warm, operational, organized, never apologetic.

## Email rules
Short sentences, one ask per email, no filler, a specific subject line, a working unsubscribe where required.

## Block list
Em or en dashes; three-adjective lists; "not just X, it is Y" constructions; hedging language; unresolved placeholder tokens; page-padding; filler openers; hollow marketing clichés.

## Warn list
Long sentences; high adjective-to-noun ratio; jargon in client-facing text; engineering shown as a line item.

## Format kill list
Logo placement errors, orphan paragraphs, text outside boxes, dark figures on dark backgrounds, blurry images.

## Outputs
Block (return with the offending lines), warn (list), or pass silently.
