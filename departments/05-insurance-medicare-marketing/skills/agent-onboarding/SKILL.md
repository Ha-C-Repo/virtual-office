# Agent onboarding

## When to use
Each time a new agent joins the branch.

## Inputs
New agent record: {{AGENT_NAME}}, resident state, start date, intended carriers.

## The twelve steps (evidence required for each)
1. Signed agreement and tax form.
2. Pre-licensing education (state dependent).
3. State exam.
4. Fingerprinting and background check.
5. License application.
6. National producer number confirmed.
7. E&O coverage bound.
8. Annual industry Medicare certification.
9. Carrier contracting, in parallel per carrier, through the agency hierarchy.
10. Ready-to-sell per carrier per plan year.
11. Non-resident licenses.
12. Continuing-education plan.

## Folder scaffold
01 Agreement; 02 Licensing and Contracting; 03 Carrier Appointments (one subfolder per carrier); 04 Marketing; 05 Production Reports; 06 Compliance; 07 Correspondence; 08 Registers; 09 Financials (CONFIDENTIAL).

## Steps
Create the scaffold. Instantiate the checklist with every step "not started" and evidence "none". Update only when a real document exists. Open tracker rows per carrier. Draft the welcome sequence in principal voice. Assemble the onboarding packet (`../../templates/onboarding-packet.md`) plus its `-INT` memo.

## Gates
Never fabricate license, producer number, appointment or certification. Welcome sequence held for principal approval. Folder 09 is never copied outward.
