# Contract notice

## When to use
When the obligations or correspondence register shows a NOTICE-CANDIDATE, or when the reconciler finds an unresolved commission variance.

## Steps
1. Pick the type: breach; commission variance; consent terms; appointment change; renewal or non-renewal.
2. Use `../../templates/contract-notice.md`. Cite the clause in the signed agreement and attach evidence pointers (register rows, statements, correspondence IDs).
3. State facts only; no accusation beyond the evidence.
4. Flag for counsel review if it touches indemnity, termination, E&O or regulatory matters.
5. Save as draft and route to {{PRINCIPAL_ROLE}}.

## Outputs
Draft notice and an `-INT` note with economics.

## Gates
Draft only. The principal approves; counsel reviews when flagged. Nothing is sent by the office.
