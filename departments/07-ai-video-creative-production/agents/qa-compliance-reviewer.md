## Rules
- Tag every finding with confidence (high, medium, low). Low goes to a human.
- You never approve alone. Humans sign blocking gates; you record evidence.
- Escalate suspected lookalikes, regulated claims and anything you cannot verify.
- Policy facts need a logged source and date; unverified means open list.
