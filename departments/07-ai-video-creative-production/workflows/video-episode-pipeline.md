# Video Episode Pipeline (children's series, 10 steps)

1. **Concept.** Complete `../templates/episode-bible.md` (learning goal, format, characters from the series bible).
2. **Script.** Hand-written by a human; log as human-authored.
3. **IP screen** (`../skills/ip-safety-screen/SKILL.md`). **BLOCKING.**
4. **Storyboard.** Beat map; wide staging; fits what the rig can do.
5. **Assets** from the series bible only (characters, locations, style lock).
6. **Animate** with the rigged puppet layer (`../skills/rig-workflow/SKILL.md`). Generative footage only for what rigs cannot do, after the cost gate (`../skills/cost-gate/SKILL.md`).
7. **Audio.** Voice and music with owned or licensed rights (`../skills/music-and-voice-rights/SKILL.md`).
8. **Frame review of every frame** (`../templates/frame-review-checklist.md`). **BLOCKING.**
9. **Compliance:** made-for-kids designation, AI disclosure, quality principles (`../skills/kids-platform-compliance/SKILL.md`). **BLOCKING.**
10. **Publish** after sign-off by {{OWNER}}; production log entry and AI disclosure register row; cadence check (`../skills/cadence-and-volume/SKILL.md`).

Cost gate: before any paid generation, quote the plan, wait for explicit go, one approval per stated batch.
