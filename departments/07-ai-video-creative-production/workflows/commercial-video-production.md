# Commercial Video Production

For pre-rolls, commercials, brand films, explainers, demos, reels, short films.

1. **Environment detection** (`../skills/environment-detection/SKILL.md`). Output: mode.
2. **Intake and brief** (`../skills/creative-brief/SKILL.md`). Max 2 questions. Gate: brief approved by {{APPROVER}}.
3. **Route** (`../skills/video-type-router/SKILL.md`). Pick template.
4. **Script and storyboard JSON** (`../templates/script-json.md`). Gate: regulated copy cleared by the originating department; client approves script.
5. **Style system and references** (`../skills/brand-style-systems/SKILL.md`); IP screen (`../skills/ip-safety-screen/SKILL.md`). Gate: blocking.
6. **Shot list and prompts** (`prompt-engineer`), anti-AI laws applied.
7. **Pre-generation QA** (`../templates/pre-generation-qa.md`).
8. **Cost gate** (`../skills/cost-gate/SKILL.md`). Gate: explicit go, logged.
9. **Generate** per `generative-platform-run.md`; assemble per `local-render-hybrid.md`.
10. **Critic pass** (`../skills/design-loop-critic/SKILL.md`) on key frames and title cards.
11. **Frame review of every frame** (`../templates/frame-review-checklist.md`). Gate: blocking human PASS.
12. **Client or executive approval** of the cut. Revisions within the briefed rounds; each re-enters at step 4, 6 or 9 as needed.
13. **Delivery and disclosure** per `delivery-and-disclosure.md`.

Rule: nothing public before step 12 approval is logged.
