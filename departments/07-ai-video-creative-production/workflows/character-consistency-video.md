# Character Consistency for Video

Mirrors `../../06-publishing/workflows/character-consistency.md` with video additions.

1. Text bible and IP screen (blocking).
2. Model sheet and expression grid; approved by a human.
3. Style lock (5 to 7 colors, one style family).
4. Location bible and plate references.
5. Generate stills first (character in each key pose and setting). Approve stills.
6. Animate: preferred route is the rig (`../skills/rig-workflow/SKILL.md`). For generative clips, use image-to-video from the approved still, one reference per subject.
7. Multi-character shots: prompt identities, positions, actions; keep shots wide and short.
8. Repair or cut drifted frames; do not chain one clip's last frame into the next prompt as the reference.
9. Frame review for continuity across the whole cut. Blocking.

Rule: the series bible is the only source of character truth.
