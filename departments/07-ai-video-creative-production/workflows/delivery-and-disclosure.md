# Delivery and Disclosure

1. Confirm blocking gates show PASS: IP screen, frame review, compliance (children's content), rights table.
2. Export final deliverables in the briefed formats; name files by project, version and date.
3. Fill the production log: stage, date, human, human input, AI role, gate.
4. Add the AI disclosure register row: asset, surface, ai_generated (video/voice/music/image), human_work_summary, disclosure_filed_where.
5. Set platform disclosure toggles (synthetic or altered content, made-for-kids) consistent with the register.
6. Executive or client approval in writing, logged.
7. Deliver or publish. For client delivery send via the agreed channel; for public posts the approver publishes or confirms.
8. Archive project files (script, prompts, references, outputs, rights docs, approvals). Move, never delete.
9. Spend reconciliation to `09-business-admin-finance-compliance`.

Gate: no public release or client delivery before step 6.
