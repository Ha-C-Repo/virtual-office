# Short Film

## When to use
Narrative or atmospheric pieces longer than an ad.

## Archetypes
- **Three-act:** setup, confrontation, resolution. One protagonist, one want, one obstacle.
- **Vignette:** a single moment explored from several angles; mood over plot.
- **Cinematic sequence:** a visual journey with a simple arc; little or no dialogue.

## Steps
1. Choose the archetype and fill `../../templates/short-film-archetypes.md`.
2. Write the script and storyboard JSON; keep to a small cast and few locations (consistency).
3. Define the style system and character references; run `../ip-safety-screen/SKILL.md`.
4. Produce through `../../workflows/commercial-video-production.md`.
5. Add sound design and music with cleared rights.
6. Frame review, then approval.

## Gates
Cost gate and frame review apply in full. Keep shots short; avoid dialogue-driven scenes that depend on lip-sync the tools cannot hold.
