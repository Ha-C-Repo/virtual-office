# IP Safety Screen (blocking)

## When to use
Before any character, title, song, voice, logo or setting enters a project, and again at frame review for generated frames.

## The six points
1. **Character lookalike:** does it resemble a protected character in shape, color scheme, costume or name?
2. **Name and title:** is it identical or confusingly similar to an existing property? Run a search and a trademark check (hand to `11-ip-patent-support` when needed).
3. **Music and melody:** is it original, licensed or generated with a license that allows this use? Does a melody echo a known tune?
4. **Voice:** is it a licensed or owned voice; no clone of a real person without written consent.
5. **Brand marks and products:** no third-party logos, packaging or product designs unless licensed.
6. **Real persons and places:** no real person's likeness without a release; avoid identifiable private property.

## Steps
1. Check `../../templates/ip-blocklist.md` (never-generate list).
2. Answer each of the six points, with evidence, in the project record.
3. Any "yes" or "unsure" stops the item; redesign or clear.
4. A human signs.

## Gates
Blocking. Unsure means no.
