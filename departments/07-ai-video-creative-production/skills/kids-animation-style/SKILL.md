# Kids Animation Style (9 laws)

## When to use
Every children's episode, loop and short.

## Laws
1. Wide, stable staging: keep characters fully in frame; avoid fast cuts and abrupt zooms.
2. Gentle pacing: one idea per beat; leave pauses for the child to respond.
3. Bright but controlled palette from the style lock; no flashing or high-contrast strobing.
4. Clear faces with readable expressions that match the line.
5. Repetition with variation: refrains, call and response, learning loops.
6. Calm sound design: warm voice, no sudden loud effects, music never masks words.
7. Safe content: no imitable danger, no scary peaks, no manipulation or pressure to buy or click.
8. One learning goal per episode, stated in the episode bible and shown through story.
9. Consistent characters and places from the series bible; no off-model frames.

## Steps
Check the storyboard against the laws before animation, and the final cut at frame review.

## Gates
A law violation found at frame review sends the shot back; it is not waived.
