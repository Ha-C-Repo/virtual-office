# Creative Brief

## When to use
Before any script. The brief is the contract for scope, audience and revisions.

## Inputs
Request from {{CLIENT_NAME}} or an internal department; brand tokens; any assets supplied.

## Steps
1. Ask at most two questions to fill gaps. Infer the rest and mark inferences clearly.
2. Complete `../../templates/creative-brief.md`: overview, objective, audience, three key messages, production specs (duration, aspect ratio, platform, engine), visual direction, must include, must avoid, assets provided, approach, revision process, credit budget table.
3. The credit budget table lists clips, attempts per clip, expected cost units, and a contingency line. This feeds the cost gate.
4. Define the revision process: number of included revision rounds, who approves, turnaround.
5. Send for sign-off by {{CLIENT_NAME}} (or {{OWNER}} internally).

## Outputs
Approved brief saved with the project.

## Gates
No script work starts before the brief is approved. Scope changes need a revised brief and a new cost-gate go.
