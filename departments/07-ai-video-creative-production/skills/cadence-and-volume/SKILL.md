# Cadence and Volume

## When to use
Planning a release calendar for any series or channel.

## Rules
1. Set a cap on uploads per week ({{MAX_UPLOADS_PER_WEEK}}) and do not exceed it, even when assets are ready.
2. Prefer fewer, better episodes with visible human creative work.
3. Vary formats across the calendar (episode, rhyme, loop, short) rather than repeating one template.
4. Credibility signals: a real creator presence, credited art direction, consistent series bible, human-written scripts, honest descriptions.
5. Shorts funnel to full episodes, not the reverse. Do not post near-duplicates.
6. Watch for platform warnings; pause releases on any flag and review policy.

## Steps
1. Build the calendar in the production log.
2. Review monthly: volume, flags, retention.

## Gates
{{OWNER}} approves the calendar. Volume increases require a policy re-check first.
