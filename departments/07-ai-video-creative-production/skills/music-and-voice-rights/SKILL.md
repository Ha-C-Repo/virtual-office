# Music and Voice Rights

## When to use
Whenever audio is added: voiceover, singing, music, effects.

## Rules
1. Music must be owned, licensed for this use and platform, or generated under a license that allows commercial use. Keep the license file and scope.
2. Voice must be a human performer under a release, a licensed synthetic voice, or the studio's own voice. No cloning of a real person without written consent.
3. Credit rule: credit performers, composers and licensors where the license or platform requires it, in the description or end card.
4. No melody or lyric that echoes a known work; screen with `../ip-safety-screen/SKILL.md`.
5. Record each audio asset in `../../../06-publishing/templates/permissions-log.md` format (item, type, rights holder, status, license scope).
6. Level and clarity: voice intelligible over music; music ducked under speech.
7. For audiobook or marketplace uses, check rules that bar synthesized narration.

## Outputs
Audio rights table attached to the production log.

## Gates
No audio asset enters the final without a cleared status.
