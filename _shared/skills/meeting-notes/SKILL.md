# Meeting notes

## When to use
Client calls, internal reviews, site meetings, interviews, kickoff and closeout meetings.

## Inputs
Transcript or notes, attendee roles (not names), agenda, template `../../templates/meeting-notes.md`.

## Steps
1. Header: date, purpose, attendee roles, source of the notes, confidence tag.
2. Decisions: each with who decided and the reason. Copy to the decision log.
3. Action items: task, owner role, due date. If a due date was not stated, write NEEDS_INPUT; do not invent one.
4. Open questions: who must answer.
5. Commitments made to outside parties: list and add to the promise list or obligations register with a date.
6. Items that might be notice candidates or scope changes: flag them for the coordinator.
7. Quote disputed statements exactly; do not paraphrase them.
8. Circulate internally after review. Outward minutes need sign-off and the internal twin.

## Outputs
Minutes, action list, register updates.

## Gates
No detail originates with the system. Low-confidence transcript passages are marked and confirmed with a person before they become commitments.

## Anti-patterns
Smoothing over disagreement. Inventing owners. Treating a transcript error as a decision.
