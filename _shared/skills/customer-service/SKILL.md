# Customer service

## When to use
Support emails, tickets, review replies, complaint letters, service-recovery messages, post-sale check-ins.

## Classes and posture
1. Allegation of concealment, safety defect, illegal conduct, discrimination, or a legal threat: WAIT. Do not draft a public reply. Escalate to the owner the same business day with the facts on file.
2. Service or follow-up complaint without an allegation: draft a reply that names the specific issue, invites direct contact, avoids defensiveness, avoids re-litigating, makes no admission of legal liability and no promise that is not authorized. BRIEF.
3. Positive feedback naming a specific action: thank the person for that specific thing. Do not reuse it as an advertisement without permission. DRAFT.
4. Generic positive or negative: short acknowledgment, low priority. DRAFT.
5. Routine question: answer from the knowledge base. If not covered, say so and escalate; do not invent.

## Steps
1. Classify and set the band.
2. Look up the account and prior contact. Never expose another customer's data.
3. Draft with the template `../../templates/service-recovery-email.md` or the knowledge base article.
4. Name the next step, the owner and the date. Any promise goes on the promise list (a due bill or equivalent) with a promise date.
5. Voice firewall; compliance check if the reply touches a regulated claim.
6. Human approves and sends public replies.
7. Track to closure. Escalate any promise that passes its date the same day.
8. Weekly digest: volume by class, response time, open items, repeated causes.

## Outputs
Reply draft, promise list entry, escalation note (`../../templates/escalation-note.md`), weekly digest.

## Gates
- Never promise a refund, credit, repair or outcome without authority.
- Never count a complaint resolved because a reply was posted.
- Never delete or suppress reviews; do not claim reviews were removed.
- AI first-response surfaces disclose that they are AI and hand to a human on request.

## Anti-patterns
Auto-replying to public comments. Debating facts in public. Closing a ticket without confirmation.
