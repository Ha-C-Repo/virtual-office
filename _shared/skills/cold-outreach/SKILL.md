# Cold outreach

## When to use
First contact with a business prospect or referral partner. Not for consumers in regulated products (use `../lead-followup/SKILL.md` and the department rules).

## Inputs
Prospect company role and segment, one recent public fact about them, the offer, one credibility line with a source, the sender role, consent and suppression lists.

## Eligibility check (before drafting)
- Is the contact a business address with a lawful basis for commercial email in this jurisdiction?
- Are they on the suppression or opt-out list? If yes, stop.
- Can you personalize with at least: company, region, one recent public fact, one relevant client type, team size? If personalization is unknowable, skip the prospect. Do not send generic text.

## Structure
1. Specific subject line: five to eight words, no tricks.
2. One observation about them, from a source.
3. The problem you solve and one credibility line.
4. A single call to action with an easy yes or no.
Body about 120 words maximum. Include sender identity, physical postal address placeholder `{{BUSINESS_ADDRESS}}`, and a working opt-out.

## Sequence pattern
Touches on days 1, 4, 8, 14, 21, 30 with a new angle each time (proof, question, useful resource, short video, direct ask, close the loop). Stop on any reply or opt-out.

## Steps
Check eligibility, personalize, draft, voice firewall, compliance review, BRIEF-band approval, human send, log in the contact register, schedule next touch.

## Outputs
Draft email(s), eligibility note, sequence calendar, log row.

## Gates
- Opt-outs update the register the same day and apply across channels.
- No false urgency, no fake "re:" subjects, no invented familiarity.
- Funnel numbers (opens, replies, bookings) are measured, not asserted.

## Anti-patterns
Generic openers. More than one ask. Claiming results you cannot source.
