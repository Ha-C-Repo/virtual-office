# Marketing content

## When to use
Social posts, newsletters, case studies, website copy, short video scripts, content calendars, campaign plans. For regulated products (Medicare, vehicles, housing, credit, children) also load the department rules file and route through the compliance reviewer.

## Inputs
Audience, offer, one proof point with a source, channel, brand voice guide, department rules, approved claims register (if any).

## Steps
1. Audience first. Name who it is for and the single action you want.
2. One idea per piece. Specificity quota: include at least one concrete fact (number, place, date, item) from a source on file.
3. Draft in the brand voice. Hook in the first line. One call to action.
4. Tag dimensions at draft time so results can be learned from: format, hook, topic, media, length, call to action.
5. Screen: claims against the substantiation register (no row, no claim), regulated-language rules, voice firewall, anonymization.
6. Approval queue: BRIEF band. A human approves each piece. Blocked drafts are logged, never queued.
7. Publish at human pace through the platform's own scheduler. One post at a time. Stop and hand to a human on any login, verification or rate warning.
8. Record the permalink in the post ledger.
9. Measure from first-party exports only, never scraped. Note any platform metric change as a series break.
10. Learn weekly and cumulatively. State plainly when the audience is too small to separate winners from noise ("not separated").

## Outputs
Draft, dimension tags, screen result, approval package, post ledger row, weekly learning note.

## Gates
- No fabricated testimonials, statistics, awards or credentials.
- Real people and real products only; generated imagery of products, buildings or people is labeled or not used, per department rules.
- Nobody auto-replies to comments or messages.
- Paid promotion is out of scope unless the owner opens it explicitly.
- Non-English copy is refused until a named reviewer exists.

## Anti-patterns
Chasing weekly winners with tiny samples. Posting from a personal profile for a business. Pre-dating posts. Mixing prep, approval, scheduling and publication in status reports.
