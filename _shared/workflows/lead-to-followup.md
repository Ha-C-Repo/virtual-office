# Workflow: Lead to Follow-up

Skills: `../skills/lead-followup/SKILL.md`, `../skills/cold-outreach/SKILL.md`, `../skills/registers/SKILL.md`.

| # | Step | Who | Gate / sign-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture the lead once; dedupe; start the speed-to-lead timer. | Records keeper | One record per lead |
| 2 | Step 0: consent check per channel against the consent register and opt-out list. | Records keeper | No row, expired or revoked: no draft; name what would cure it |
| 3 | Pick the channel by consent, not convenience. | Author agent | |
| 4 | Draft a reply naming the actual item and its actual price or status from the record. | Author agent | Verify, do not generate |
| 5 | Voice check and compliance review. | QA, compliance | Regulated channels follow the department rules |
| 6 | HUMAN SIGN-OFF until the owner trusts the voice; then low-risk replies may be DRAFT band. | Owner role | AI never sends, dials or auto-replies |
| 7 | Human sends. Log touch and outcome. | Human, records keeper | |
| 8 | Cadence: call within 24 hours, day 3, day 7, monthly; text at most weekly with opt-out; email welcome within 24 hours then at most weekly. | Coordinator | Department rules override |
| 9 | Record promises on the promise list with dates. | Records keeper | Unlisted promise = escalate |
| 10 | Opt-out: update the register the same day, all channels. | Records keeper | Permanent |
| 11 | 30-day expiry sweep; stale leads closed with a reason, not deleted. | Records keeper | |
| 12 | Weekly: speed-to-lead, show rate, conversions; honest limits stated. | Coordinator | `../skills/reporting/SKILL.md` |
